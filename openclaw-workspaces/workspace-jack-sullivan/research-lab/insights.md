## Email Outreach

### What's Working
- **Soft subject lines** ("Quick question about...") feel less salesy
- **Qualification-focused copy** — asking about their situation vs. pitching
- **CC'ing Chris** — keeps him in the loop without extra work

### What Needs Testing
- Subject line A/B test in progress (Venetian Bay)
- Morning vs. afternoon send times
- Shorter emails (< 100 words) vs. current length

### Early Signals
- 2 "not interested" replies out of 140 sent = 1.4% reply rate (Day 1)
- Both were clear opt-outs, not hostile — tone seems right
- 2 bounces (~1.4%) — normal for cold lists

## Messaging Angles

### By Lead Type
| Lead Type | Best Angle | Avoid |
|-----------|------------|-------|
| Absentee | "Managing from afar" | Assuming they want to sell |
| Pre-foreclosure | "Exploring options" | Mentioning foreclosure directly |
| Expired | "Fresh approach" | Criticizing previous agent |
| Divorce/Probate | "Life transitions" | NEVER mention divorce/death |

### Copy Rules (Learned)
- No dashes in any copy (Chris preference)
- Keep emails under 150 words
- Always end with a question
- Sign as Chris for credibility, Jack for lead gen

## Sequence Optimization

### Current Hypothesis
- 10 touches over 30 days is the sweet spot for absentee
- 25 touches over 30 days for pre-foreclosure (higher urgency)
- Stop sequence immediately on ANY reply (positive or negative)

### Open Questions
- What's the optimal gap between email touches? (Testing 3 days)
- At what touch # do most responses come?
- Does adding SMS increase response or annoy?
