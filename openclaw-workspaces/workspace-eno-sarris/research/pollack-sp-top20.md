# Nick Pollack's Top 20 SP Breakdown (2026)
*Source: PitcherList - "Top 400 Starting Pitchers for Fantasy Baseball 2026"*

---

## TIER 1 — STUD

### 1. Garrett Crochet (BOS, LHP)
**Strengths:**
- 205.1 IP in 2025 — proved he can handle workhorse volume
- Sinker/cutter/sweeper combo overwhelms LHB
- Four-seamer/cutter combo earns tons of strikes and whiffs
- Generates worse contact than velocity would suggest
- Elite ratios: sub-3.00 ERA, ~1.00 WHIP, 30% K rate
- Best Win potential of the top 3 (Boston offense)

**Weaknesses:**
- Sweeper is a two-strike offering only — needs extra weapon early in counts
- Can struggle locating four-seamer/cutter at times
- Pushed hard through playoffs in 2025 — some injury worry

**Nick's Take:** "If you're aiming to draft an SP early, take the third of Crochet/Skenes/Skubal. If you have to choose first, chase the pitcher with highest chance of Wins — Crochet."

---

### 2. Paul Skenes (PIT, RHP)
**Strengths:**
- Three pitches at 94+ mph with different movement — "bonkers"
- Elite command disguised as power pitcher
- Generates weak contact to RHB despite average SwStr on four-seamer
- Four-seamer carves LHB at 18% SwStr rate
- Back-to-back sub-2.00 ERA seasons
- Only 23 years old — hasn't mastered his approach yet (ceiling remains)

**Weaknesses:**
- Mediocre feel for spin
- Four-seamer SwStr rate to RHB is just average
- Lacks depth offerings to truly shut down LHB
- Slightly less volume than Crochet/Skubal

**Nick's Take:** "He's a crafty/command pitcher who happens to have elite velocity. His quality floor is so high."

---

### 3. Tarik Skubal (DET, LHP)
**Strengths:**
- SWATCH (command pitcher) who throws 97+ mph, touches 100
- Elite changeup
- Stellar control
- Consistently goes 6+ frames
- Could go 200 IP / 250 K / sub-2.50 ERA / sub-1.00 WHIP

**Weaknesses:**
- Slider only generates 7% SwStr to LHB — needs improvement
- Slight regression possible on velocity/execution

**Nick's Take:** "You know Tarik is dope. If you want him as SP #1, go ahead."

---

## TIER 2 — AGA 1 (Aces Gonna Ace)

### 4. Bryan Woo (SEA, RHP)
**Strengths:**
- Four-seamer/sinker combo = one of the best fastball duos in baseball
- Elite command, 95/96 mph velocity
- One of the flattest attack angles in baseball — can go 70% fastballs
- 30 starts / 187 IP in 2025 after injury concerns in 2024
- Great park (Seattle), winning team, long leash
- Clear path to 200+ IP and K's with sub-3.00 ERA, sub-1.00 WHIP
- Room to grow if secondaries improve

**Weaknesses:**
- Slider and sweeper lack consistent location
- Secondaries not overwhelming — relies heavily on fastballs

**Nick's Take:** "I have him as my #4 SP, which goes against many experts. His fastball foundation is as good as any in baseball."

---

### 5. Yoshinobu Yamamoto (LAD, RHP)
**Strengths:**
- Elite command pitcher
- Filthy splitter for strikeouts
- 95 mph four-seamer with flat 1.6 HAVAA
- Curveball has 96th percentile total break, 40% CSW to RHB
- Cutter is fantastic inside to LHB
- Sinker is 99th percentile inside location (80%+ inside to RHB)
- Suffocating attack to LHB

**Weaknesses:**
- Six-man rotation caps volume at ~170 IP
- Walk rate spiked (8 walks on sinker to RHB in 3-ball counts)
- Four-seamer allows 50%+ ICR to RHB — sits over plate too often
- .236 BABIP to RHB won't repeat — WHIP will rise
- 30 starts included Japan series — 28 more realistic

**Nick's Take:** "Elite quality per inning, but capped volume ceiling. I question how aggressively he should be drafted."

---

### 6. Max Fried (NYY, LHP)
**Strengths:**
- "As safe as they come"
- Cutters inside to RHB carry genuine surprise cut
- Sinker to LHB is a weak-contact machine
- Foundation of NYY rotation (Cole/Rodon sidelined) — Wins will flow
- 75 mph curveball with 99th percentile drop = elite putaway pitch
- Ratio rock with great home run suppression

**Weaknesses:**
- Won't wow with strikeouts (not K-per-inning guy)
- Changeup SwStr dropped from 19-20% to 14% vs RHB
- Sweeper is not elite to LHB — sub-60% strikes

**Nick's Take:** "Pair him with a K-forward arm and enjoy the quality ratio floor with a boatload of dubs."

---

### 7. Cristopher Sánchez (PHI, LHP)
**Strengths:**
- Added velocity tick + 3 extra inches of vertical depth on sinker
- Sinker now generates 12% SwStr to LHB (career high was 8%)
- Slider tightened — doubled putaway rate to LHB, SwStr jumped 16% → 23%
- Changeup exploded: 39% putaway to LHB, 31% SwStr
- 200+ frames potential
- Phillies context = wins

**Weaknesses:**
- Putaway rates likely won't sustain — expect 23-24% K rate (down from 26%)
- WHIP likely rises north of 1.10, ERA close to 3.00
- Forearm soreness scare in 2025

**Nick's Take:** "Assign whatever regression you'd like — at least we can agree his quality-per-start will still be elite."

---

### 8. Logan Gilbert (SEA, RHP)
**Strengths:**
- Elite 7.6 feet of extension
- Four-seamer at 95/96 mph with great tunneling
- Slider is elite when located properly
- Added splitter as #3 pitch — excellent putaway offering
- Finished 2025 hot: 11 ER, 30% K rate in final 7 starts
- Forearm strain (Grade 1) appears behind him
- Mariners will let him go all year

**Weaknesses:**
- Slider location was terrible in 2025 — thrown middle of zone (86th percentile, bad)
- Curveball shows plus at times but too inconsistent
- Essentially four-seamer/slider with splitter for strikeouts

**Nick's Take:** "If he can turn the corner to keep his slider down as in 2024, you have another stud season ahead."

---

### 9. Hunter Greene (CIN, RHP)
**Strengths:**
- #1 PLV four-seamer in baseball
- Sat 97+ in 2024, added two ticks to 99+ in 2025
- Slider gained 3 ticks to 89/90 mph
- Improved strike-throwing with slider
- Slider down-and-in fuels strikeouts vs LHB (not the splitter)

**Weaknesses:**
- Volume concern: career high 150 IP, sandwiched by sub-115 IP seasons
- Groin strain bothered him twice in 2025 (not arm, but still)
- Splitter is 50% strike rate with bad putaway — not the weapon

**Nick's Take:** "140-150 IP seems fair. In shallow leagues, Greene + wire replacement = easily Top 10 SP."

---

### 10. Hunter Brown (HOU, RHP)
**Strengths:**
- Four-seamer/sinker command is incredible
- Added velocity tick to ~97 mph
- Four-seamer shifted from up-middle to up-away vs RHB — ICR dropped 47% → 35%
- Sinker has 72% inside location to RHB with 26% ICR (!!)
- Curveball became major putaway pitch (28% putaway rate)
- Stable volume arm

**Weaknesses:**
- 2025 stats don't add up: 28% K rate with sub-13% SwStr rate
- Sub-.270 BABIP with 50% GB rate is too low — will regress
- 82% LOB rate = regression coming
- LHB are still a problem — four-seamer overperformed, changeup only 54% strikes
- Nick is lower on him than consensus

**Nick's Take:** "I don't see his ceiling as safe as Woo, Greene, Gilbert. However, regression is still miles away from harmful."

## TIER 3 — AGA 2

### 11. Shohei Ohtani (LAD, RHP)
**Strengths:**
- "Fantasy sleeper" — ADP dictated by hitter value, pitching undervalued
- Same elite pitcher of old with improved slider
- Slight velocity uptick
- 30%+ K rate, sub-1.10 WHIP for 4th straight SP season
- Full rehab + no WBC = ready for every-6-days role
- New slider could get larger spotlight vs LHB

**Weaknesses:**
- Six-man rotation limits volume
- Projections show 110-120 IP (Nick thinks 150 possible like Rays did with Rasmussen)

**Nick's Take:** "30% K rate with elite ERA/WHIP for a winning team. I want this everywhere."

---

### 12. Logan Webb (SFG, RHP)
**Strengths:**
- Durability is unparalleled — 5 straight sub-3.50 ERA seasons
- Goes deep into games consistently
- Low 3s ERA, ~200 K's, double-digit Wins = floor
- Absurd groundball rate

**Weaknesses:**
- WHIP concerns (could push 1.20+)
- Strikeout uptick (26%+) may not sustain — expect ~23%
- Changeup 30% putaway and sweeper 27% putaway likely to regress
- 3rd percentile BABIP luck last year

**Nick's Take:** "Webb's durability is unparalleled. Even a K-per-inning returns 200 punchouts."

---

### 13. Joe Ryan (MIN, RHP)
**Strengths:**
- 99th percentile HAVAA on four-seamer
- Solid extension, fantastic ability to keep it up in zone
- Sinker usage increasing (15%+) with more horizontal movement
- ERA above 3.60 just once in 4 seasons
- Incredible WHIP marks
- Nearly 200 K's twice in 3 seasons
- One of the safer SP 1.5/2 options

**Weaknesses:**
- Susceptible to longball (16 HR off heater in 2025, 19 in 2023)
- Lacks filthy #2 pitch vs RHB or LHB
- Team context (Minnesota) may affect morale/production

**Nick's Take:** "When your safe arm can strikeout 200 batters, you should have a massive grin."

---

### 14. Freddy Peralta (NYM, RHP)
**Strengths:**
- Lowest ERA of career in 2025, sub-1.10 WHIP
- Four-seamer has elite HAVAA with unexpected movement at 94/95 mph
- Shifted on rubber toward 3B — getting more cut on heater
- Curveball became favorite putaway to both sides
- Changeup increased usage to RHB
- 3 straight 30-start seasons

**Weaknesses:**
- HOTEL season: 85%+ LOB, .256 BABIP did heavy lifting
- Four-seamer dropped to 10% SwStr to LHB
- Can't get pitches over plate consistently
- New park (Citi Field) less pitcher-friendly than Miller Park for K's

**Nick's Take:** "Looking like one of the more stable options as SP #2."

---

## TIER 4 — AGA INJURY RISK

### 15. Jacob deGrom (TEX, RHP)
**Strengths:**
- 172.2 IP in first healthy-elbow season
- Still phenomenal when pitching
- Excellent command pitcher

**Weaknesses:**
- Near 38 years old with injury history
- Four-seamer SwStr dropped 18% → 12%
- Slider SwStr dropped 33% → 18% vs RHB
- Missed August with shoulder fatigue
- TJS honeymoon could be ending

**Nick's Take:** "When he's on the field, he'll be great. Go with your gut on health."

---

### 16. Cole Ragans (KCR, LHP)
**Strengths:**
- SWATCH with phenomenal fastball
- Four-seamer has most total break of any four-seamer in baseball
- Absurd run + rise at 95+ mph
- Obliterates RHB with changeup and heater
- New slider added for LHB
- Can flirt with 30% K rate
- Top 5 potential when healthy

**Weaknesses:**
- Groin strain + 3-month rotator cuff strain in 2025
- Slider declined — hard contact spiked, sub-60% strike rate
- LHB approach is problem — lacks four-seamer whiffs or changeup trust
- Two-pitch LHB approach relies on slider location (spotty)

**Nick's Take:** "One of the only arms who can perform at the level of the Holy Trinity in Tier 1."

---

### 17. George Kirby (SEA, RHP)
**Strengths:**
- Sinker/breakers are "ridiculous" — sweep and depth at 84-88 mph
- Could flip switch and become one of best pitchers in baseball
- Ceiling = Top 5 SP
- Few walks
- BSB approach in September looked promising

**Weaknesses:**
- Recurring shoulder problems
- Sinkers thrown up-and-away to RHB instead of inside
- Sliders/curves kept too high — allowing hard contact
- Four-seamer overused early (72%) but underused two-strike (16%)
- Mental approach is hurting him

**Nick's Take:** "The floor is missing significant time. The ceiling is Top 5 SP. High risk/high reward."

---

### 18. Tyler Glasnow (LAD, RHP)
**Strengths:**
- Top 5 SP in baseball when pitching
- Four-seamer cut action destroys LHB
- Curveball is one of most effective two-strike pitches (100th percentile extension)
- Worth it in shallow leagues (elite innings + replacement)

**Weaknesses:**
- Will get hurt
- Slider yanked gloveside too often vs RHB — zone/strike rate tanked to ~50%
- Walk problems stem from slider issues

**Nick's Take:** "I'm drafting a lot more Glasnow than I thought. Elite moments when you have them."

---

### 19. Chris Sale (ATL, LHP)
**Strengths:**
- Still phenomenal when pitching
- Slider/four-seamer combo is that good
- Sinker saved for 15% vs LHB — had 5.94 PLV (elite)

**Weaknesses:**
- Fractured rib diving for ball — missed 2+ months
- 150+ IP ceiling unrealistic
- Refuses to throw changeups

**Nick's Take:** "I'd rather take a more secure arm for quality volume."

---

## TIER 5 — AGA HOLLY

### 20. Kyle Bradish (BAL, RHP)
**Strengths:**
- Returned from TJS in late August and picked up where left off
- Slider is one of best in baseball — major sweep and drop at 87 mph
- Curveball has rare -13" vert at 84+ mph
- Two elite breaking balls = strikeouts will flow
- Feel for inside sinkers to RHB is huge plus
- 168.2 IP in 2023 proves workload capability
- Abbreviated 2025 = extra rehab, should be 100% for April
- Orioles solid team, good park, offense should score

**Weaknesses:**
- Only 71 IP in last two seasons (TJS recovery)
- Four-seamer has questionable ball-in-play rate — batters lace it
- Needs to command four-seamer inside to LHB better

**Nick's Take:** "Workload concerns are overblown. Feel confident drafting him as a workhorse."

---

## KEY TAKEAWAYS

1. **Holy Trinity:** Crochet/Skenes/Skubal — take whichever falls to you
2. **Nick's Sleeper:** Bryan Woo at #4 — fastball foundation is elite
3. **Volume Concerns:** Yamamoto, Greene, Ohtani all capped by situation/health
4. **Regression Candidates:** Hunter Brown, Freddy Peralta (HOTEL/BABIP luck)
5. **Injury Risks with Elite Upside:** Ragans, Kirby, Glasnow, deGrom
6. **Safe Floor Plays:** Fried, Webb, Joe Ryan, Sánchez
