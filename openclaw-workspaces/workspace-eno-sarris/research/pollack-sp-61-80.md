# Nick Pollack's SP Rankings 61-80 (2026)
*Source: PitcherList - "Top 400 Starting Pitchers for Fantasy Baseball 2026"*

---

## TIER 12 — TOBY UPSIDE 1 (Cont'd)

### 61. Zac Gallen (ARI, RHP)
**Strengths:**
- Back on one-year deal to bet on himself
- Four-seamer arguably best called strike four-seamer in majors
- Curveball will ensure 24%+ K rate
- Great spot in Arizona to produce

**Weaknesses:**
- Four-seamer has vert with cut-action that harms him vs RHB
- Changeup needs to find more strikes vs LHB
- Slider can't eclipse 60% strike rate to RHB
- Walks increased last two seasons

**Nick's Take:** "There are small areas of improvement that would take Gallen from waiver wire arm to SP #3/#4. You'll know early if this works out."

## TIER 13 — INJURY STASH 1

### 62. Brandon Woodruff (MIL, RHP)
**Strengths:**
- 32% K rate and blissful ratios in 64 frames of 2025
- Unique three-fastball attack (cutter, four-seamer, sinker) — all look similar, go different directions
- Four-seamer had 30%+ putaway rate, 23% SwStr (absurd outliers)
- Solid floor even with velocity dip

**Weaknesses:**
- Two-tick velocity drop (95+ → ~93 mph)
- IL again in September with lat strain
- Four-seamer stats are regression candidates
- LHB attack is suspect

**Nick's Take:** "He'll help your teams, but not at the same level and for an uncertain amount of time."

---

### 63. Gerrit Cole (NYY, RHP) — IL STASH #1
**Strengths:**
- Slider issue is deployment, not stuff (27% putaway in 2022 → 12% in return)
- Cutter addition provides new count pitch to free slider as finisher
- TJS recovery tends to be straightforward for elite arms
- June return realistic

**Weaknesses:**
- 7% walk rate was anomalous (arm likely hurt)
- Coming off TJS

**Nick's Take:** "Among all IL stashes, Cole is the one I'd target first."

---

### 64. Zack Wheeler (PHI, RHP) — IL STASH
**Strengths:**
- "Dope and makes us feel dope" — would be Tier 1 if healthy
- Announced retirement end of 2027 = motivated to give everything
- Part of core four IL stashes (Cole, Wheeler, Rodon, Jones)

**Weaknesses:**
- Mild Thoracic Outlet Syndrome surgery
- ETA ~June 1st (highly unlikely April)
- Uncertainty about effect of surgery

**Nick's Take:** "I cannot envision a version of Wheeler that is detrimental to your teams once he returns."

---

### 65. Carlos Rodón (NYY, LHP) — IL STASH
**Strengths:**
- Talent never the question
- Productive when he's back

**Weaknesses:**
- Bone spur procedure, mid-May timeline (likely drifting later)
- Non-TJS IL starts have frustrating history of timelines pushing back
- Already fights his command
- Changeup to RHB was inconsistent even when healthy

**Nick's Take:** "Value at pick 200, an overpay much earlier."

---

### 66. Jared Jones (PIT, RHP) — IL STASH
**Strengths:**
- Skillset better than Chandler
- Four-seamer was Fan4+ darling
- Slider missed far more bats than Chandler's
- Sinker getting introduced, flirting with more changeups
- Could return ~June 1st, pitch four straight months
- "Easily inside Top 30 SP a week after he returns"

**Weaknesses:**
- Already on 60-day IL (Internal Brace UCL surgery)
- Earliest return May 25th

**Nick's Take:** "Elite talent when on the mound. Have him circled as IL stash among Wheeler, Cole, and Rodon."

---

### 67. Spencer Schwellenbach (ATL, RHP) — IL STASH
**Strengths:**
- Wide arsenal with multiple whiff pitches
- Flat attack angle, great extension, 96 mph heater
- Ace potential when healthy

**Weaknesses:**
- 60-day IL to start year with elbow inflammation (June at earliest)
- Fractured elbow due to increased velocity in 2025
- May return with lower velocity = 100th percentile zone-rate fastball getting laced

**Nick's Take:** "There's an ace in here, but how much will we get? If there's haze, he fades."

### 68. Shane Bieber (TOR, RHP) — IL STASH
**Strengths:**
- Stellar return from TJS in August — looked like classic Bieber
- Slider at 85/86 mph, huge two-plane curve at 82/83 mph
- Command to elevate 17" vert was legit
- "Signature Holly" when healthy

**Weaknesses:**
- Forearm fatigue revealed after taking QO
- Season debut delayed (unclear how long)

**Nick's Take:** "Those innings we get from Bieber are highly likely to be productive. A gamble I'd take in later rounds."

## TIER 14 — TOBY UPSIDE 2

### 69. Matthew Boyd (CHC, LHP)
**Strengths:**
- First 20 starts: 2.20 ERA, 1.01 WHIP, 23% K rate, 11-3
- Fantastic changeup for RHB with four-seamer PLV loves
- Sweeper kills LHB
- Workhorse on great team

**Weaknesses:**
- Final 11 outings: 5.16 ERA, 1.25 WHIP, 18% K rate
- Four-seamer leaked to middle of plate in 2H (60% ICR)
- Changeup walks spiked in 2H (17 walks in 11 games vs 9 in first half)

**Nick's Take:** "The second half was rough due to command failures. I don't expect warts to go untreated. Another workhorse season likely."

---

### 70. Luis Castillo (SEA, RHP)

- Still produces solid season-long marks

**Weaknesses:**
- Velocity declined four straight seasons
- Lost over an inch of vert since 2023
- 5.4 feet extension = worst in all of baseball
- Changeup and slider both dropped below 60% strike rate (first time ever for changeup)
- Had 3.94 ERA, 1.29 WHIP entering September

## TIER 15 — HIPSTER 1

### 73. Andrew Painter (PHI, RHP)
**Strengths:**
- 96/97 mph cut-action four-seamer
- 90 mph cutter is filthy
- Two-plane 83 mph curveball
- 87 mph slider/sweeper

**Weaknesses:**
- Struggled in Triple-A after first two months (5.40 ERA, 1.55 WHIP to end year)
- Fastball lost 1.5" vert (17" → 15.5")
- Keeps tweaking slider, curve, change — not a finished product
- Low extension, steep attack angle, mediocre vert
- May need more time in minors

**Nick's Take:** "I'm still waiting to see the Painter that was promised. Worried he won't be there in spring."

---

### 74. Spencer Strider (ATL, RHP) — FADE
**Strengths:**
- Still has slider
- Can miss bats

**Weaknesses:**
- Four-seamer lost 1-2 ticks, 2" vert, and flat attack angle
- SwStr dropped from 15% to 8%
- Slider sub-60% strike rate without doom of four-seamer
- No pitcher has ever: had elite four-seamer → elbow surgery → worse four-seamer → regained elite four-seamer

**Nick's Take:** "Turning an average fastball into an elite fastball over an off-season — I can't buy into that. Give me something else."

### 75. Logan Henderson (MIL, RHP)
**Strengths:**
- Really good four-seamer (93 mph, flat, stellar precision upstairs, surprising vert at 33° arm angle)
- 70%+ strikes to both LHB and RHB
- Cutter had success with "Cannibal McSanchez" to LHB
- Working on curveball

**Weaknesses:**
- Changeup is erratic (kick-change, can be deadly but scattered)
- Right flexor strain in August
- May stay in minors to start

**Nick's Take:** "If he breaks camp with the squad, I'd target him. That four-seamer is too good."

- Array of changeups, big drop 83/84 mph slider, hard two-plane 82/83 mph curve

**Weaknesses:**
- May be delayed if others pitch well in camp

**Nick's Take:** "You should pay attention to Snelling. When he gets the call, you better jump for him."

### 77. Braxton Ashcraft (PIT, RHP)
**Strengths:**
- 92 mph slider is one of best in baseball (70%+ strikes, 33% CSW to LHB, 21% SwStr to RHB)
- 84/85 mph curveball is gorgeous
- Sinker produces weak contact to RHB

**Weaknesses:**
- Four-seamer is Empty Velocity (4.63 PLV despite 96/97 mph and 62% strikes)
- LHB are biggest concern — fastballs failing
- 2025 came mostly in relief/limited starts (never 75+ pitches)
- Command and arsenal depth concerns

**Nick's Take:** "The slider is disgusting but I'm concerned we have a HIPSTER on our hands."

### 79. Jack Flaherty (DET, RHP)
**Strengths:**
- Sub-30° arm angle makes 15"+ vert actually good
- 1.6 HAVAA
- Curve and slider can miss tons of bats
- 27%+ K rate last year

**Weaknesses:**
- Leaning into low fastballs (99th percentile loLoc, 1st percentile hiLoc) — flat attack angle betrays approach
- More hard contact than ever
- Velocity dipped
- Curve and slider returned fewer strikes to LHB

**Nick's Take:** "Flaherty could go on another dominant stretch with another tweak. Don't rule him out just yet."

## TIER 16 — TOBY UPSIDE 3

### 80. Kodai Senga (NYM, RHP)
**Strengths:**
- "Ghost Fork" splitter ensures 20%+ K rate
- Cutter returned 70%+ strikes and low ICR in 2023
- April/May 2025 was productive

**Weaknesses:**
- Cutter wasn't thrown as often in 2025, couldn't fan batters with it
- Hamstring strain killed rhythm in 2H
- Four-seamer likely won't return to 95/96 mph
- Needs to reduce four-seamer usage with more cutters, sinkers, sliders, curves
- WHIP liability

**Nick's Take:** "On the edge of Toby and Holly. Possible HIPSTER, but I'd grab him as a late flier hoping he finds health and rhythm."
