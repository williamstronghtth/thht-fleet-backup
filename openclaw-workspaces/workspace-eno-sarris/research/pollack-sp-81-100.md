## TIER 16 — TOBY UPSIDE 3 (Cont'd)

### 81. Joey Cantillo (CLE, LHP)
**Strengths:**
- Pure SWATCH — changeup is everything (28% SwStr to RHB)
- 99th percentile extension
- Curveball has -21" drop (99th percentile) with rare feel for it (60%+ strikes)
- 2.48 ERA, 1.12 WHIP, 25% K in 10 starts (2H 2025)

**Weaknesses:**
- Four-seamer is not good (high attack angle, minimal movement, hard contact)
- .228 BABIP with 44% grounders won't repeat
- No sinker (difficult at 55° arm angle), slider lacks drop
- LHB attack is a problem

**Nick's Take:** "Be ready when he gets his next chance. That changeup is destined to destroy RHB."

### 82. Bailey Ober (MIN, RHP)

- Two-month stretch of 2.43 ERA in 11 starts after first outing

- Was routinely drafted Top 40 entering 2025
- Starting opening weekend = quick hold/drop decision

**Weaknesses:**
- Lost 1-2 ticks on four-seamer
- Hip injury plagued season
- Questionable WHIP and K rate

**Nick's Take:** "Consider Ober in final rounds as a flier you can move on from before end of opening weekend if fastball fails to hit 91 mph."

## TIER 17 — LATE UPSIDE

### 92. Jack Leiter (TEX, RHP)
**Strengths:**
- 97+ mph four-seamer at 7 feet extension
- Great break on 87/88 mph slider
- Kick-change, solid curveball
- Final 7 starts had just 6% walk rate

**Weaknesses:**
- Command is the problem
- Zero offerings to RHB or LHB returned 15%+ SwStr
- Four-seamers and sliders casually thrown over plate
- Changeup only 54% strike rate to LHB

**Nick's Take:** "HIPSTER staple. Atypical for a pitcher to suddenly spot pitches after large sample of failing to do so."

### 93. Cristian Javier (HOU, RHP) — SLEEPER
