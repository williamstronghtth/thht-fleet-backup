## TIER 8 — CHERRY BOMB BUT MORESO (Cont'd)

### 41. Chase Burns (CIN, RHP)
**Strengths:**
- 98/99 mph four-seamer with whiff-heavy slider (Greene comp)
- Elite pitch separation — four-seamers up-and-armside, sliders down-and-gloveside
- Skilled flamethrower who can execute
- Changeup already looks like legit #3 pitch (60%+ strikes, 17% SwStr to LHB)
- Better command than standard flamethrower

**Weaknesses:**
- Cincinnati park is rough for four-seamer/slider arms (HR prone)
- Steep attack angle makes 17-18" vert more hittable than expected
- Small sample, no full season track record

**Nick's Take:** "Every time I look at Burns, I find myself more convinced. Did I just convince myself to draft Burns? Oh jeez, I guess I did."

## TIER 10 — HOLLY I FEEL A LITTLE WORSE ABOUT

### 45. Bryce Miller (SEA, RHP)
**Strengths:**
- Four-seamer still legit (if he pushes to 96 mph)
- Curveball has plenty of break at 83 mph
- Sweeper with 17" sweep and 10" drop at 83 mph
- Cutter has separation from four-seamer
- Home starts in Seattle help
- Real stud potential if he trusts secondaries

**Weaknesses:**
- Bone spurs in elbow (PRP + cortisone injection)
- Command issues on curve/sweeper to RHB
- Doesn't use cutter enough
- 68% LOB rate, high HR rate, 9% walk rate in 2025 = "my elbow is annoying me"

**Nick's Take:** "2024 seems like the peak, though I don't believe we've seen his best skillset yet. The secondaries move too much at 83 mph to fail."

---

### 46. Edward Cabrera (CHC, RHP)
**Strengths:**
- Traded to Cubs — great fit with Tyler Zombro/Tread Athletics
- Wins easier, Cubs defense among best in league
- Lowered arm angle, switched from repugnant four-seamer (54% strike) to sinker (71% strike)
- 88/89 mph slider working inside edge to LHB beautifully
- Walk rate improved via pitch-level strike rate

**Weaknesses:**
- Elbow sprain summer 2025
- Never produced season above 140 IP (2025's 137 was first over 100)
- Sinker gets demolished by LHB

**Nick's Take:** "I love the adjustments. He won't hurt you. Just need volume to see it through."

### 47. Andrew Abbott (CIN, LHP)
**Strengths:**
- Improved pitcher — moved closer to 1B, more cross-body delivery
- Changeup trusted more (25% usage, 63% strikes) — freed up heater
- Curveball found more strikes
- Sweeper gained depth, tightened precision — 67% strikes, 22% SwStr, 30%+ usage to LHB
- Developing cutter to RHB showing promise

**Weaknesses:**
- HR luck likely to regress (only 5 off four-seamer vs double-digits prior years)
- 80%+ LOB rate = regression coming
- ERA likely well north of 3.00

**Nick's Take:** "Abbott's 2025 wasn't a fluke in its entirety. The arsenal improved and displayed skills that justified plenty of his success."

### 48. Shota Imanaga (CHC, LHP)
**Strengths:**
- Near 2.0 HR/9 still had 3.71 ERA (low walks, flyball pitcher, low BABIP)
- 0.99 WHIP with few runners on base
- WHIP benefit outweighs ERA concerns
- Workhorse for winning team

**Weaknesses:**
- Four-seamer down to 90/91 mph in April, never recovered
- Lost over an inch of vert by second half — 12 of 31 HR in final 6 games
- Putaway rates dropped (four-seamer 17% → 12%, splitter down 6 points)
- Hamstring injury may have caused vert drop

**Nick's Take:** "With positive regression expected in HRs and putaway rates, I anticipate improved 2026. Great SP #3/4."

### 49. Aaron Nola (PHI, RHP)
**Strengths:**
- K-BB% essentially the same as always
- HOTEL was horrifically unlucky (62% LOB, 31% HR/FB, 19th percentile BABIP)
- Four-seamer commanded stupidly well to LHB (33% ICR, .163 avg)
- Curveball is still "Sir Dope McGee"

**Weaknesses:**

**Nick's Take:** "Live Every Day Like Nola Day. His skills are intact. Go for it."

## TIER 12 — TOBY UPSIDE 1

**Weaknesses:**
- Curveball can't hit 60% strikes to LHB (tug it too far into opposite box)

**Nick's Take:** "Too much to ask him to replicate after good fortune, clear cracks in armor, and history of low volume."

### 56. Noah Cameron (KCR, LHP) ⭐ OUR GUY
**Strengths:**
- Stellar secondary command — sliders, cutters, curves to LHB; changeup to RHB
- Commands cutter inside, change away
- Curveball effective as putaway to RHB
- 2.99 ERA, 1.10 WHIP in 2025

**Weaknesses:**
- .241 BABIP, 84% LOB rate = HOTEL (won't repeat)
- Four-seamer is 92 mph with sub-60% strikes, 5% SwStr — not a great pitch
- 20% K rate is low for 12-13% SwStr (should go up as BABIP does)
- Four-seamer 97th percentile SIP%, 87th percentile BIP% — we don't want this pitch in play

**Nick's Take:** "Command leads the way and could combat the clear regression of his HOTEL. A steady Toby with upside for Holly."

### 57. Gavin Williams (CLE, RHP) ⭐ WE TRADED HIM

- 96/97 mph four-seamer as foundation when paired with cutters and breakers

**Weaknesses:**

- Can't trust 60%+ strike rates on secondaries or 70% on fastball

### 59. Ryne Nelson (ARI, RHP) ⭐ OUR GUY
**Strengths:**
- Highest favored four-seamer by any SP in majors
- Elite vert with lower arm angle than expected + above-average extension at 95/96 mph
- 2.89 ERA, 1.07 WHIP, 22% K in 19 starts after securing rotation spot
- Six QS in final seven outings

**Weaknesses:**
- Cannot find proper complement pitch — tried cutter, slider, curve, change
- Massive reduction in H/9 led to 1.07 WHIP (won't stick with same approach)
- Near 50% ICR to RHB — batters expecting four-seamers, not punished enough
- Putaway rate beneath caliber of pitch
- If no #2 pitch = 4.00+ ERA, 1.25+ WHIP territory

**Nick's Take:** "If Nelson finds a proper #2 pitch, he could be Top 25 overnight. If he doesn't, waiver-wire worthy ratios for 12-teamers."

### 60. Joe Musgrove (SDP, RHP)
**Strengths:**
- Command can get him deep into counts
- Curve and sweeper when on = productive
- Holly potential with K per inning

**Weaknesses:**
- Fastballs are rough — below average movement, last seen at 93 mph
- Will be cutter city with plenty of breakers
- Lots of hard contact when curve/sweeper isn't there
- Command usually takes time to return after missing so much time

**Nick's Take:** "Don't draft expecting 2023/2024. Draft hoping he can go 6 frames early without destroying ratios."
