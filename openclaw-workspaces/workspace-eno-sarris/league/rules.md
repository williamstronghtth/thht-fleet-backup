## Key Strategic Implications

1. **Strikeouts hurt hitters** — contact-oriented bats have extra value
2. **QS are massive** (+8) — workload SPs are premium
3. **Saves worth +5** — closers valuable, but volatile
4. **Volume matters** — more ABs/IP = more points
5. **SB +2, CS -1** — aggressive baserunning slightly net positive
