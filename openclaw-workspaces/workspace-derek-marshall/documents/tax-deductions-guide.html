SECTION 179: THE BIG ONE

Section 179 lets you deduct the FULL purchase price of qualifying equipment in the year you buy it — instead of spreading it over many years.

2026 Limits

ItemLimit
Maximum Section 179 Deduction~$1,200,000
Phase-out Threshold~$3,000,000
Business Use Requirement50%+ for business

What Qualifies for Section 179

ItemQualifies?Notes
Trucks over 6,000 lbs GVWR✅ YESF-250, F-350, 2500, 3500, etc.
Trailers✅ YESDump trailers, enclosed trailers
Tools & Equipment✅ YESNail guns, compressors, ladders, etc.
Office Equipment✅ YESComputers, printers, phones
Software✅ YESAccounting software, CRM, etc.
Passenger vehicles under 6,000 lbs⚠️ LIMITEDCapped at ~$12,000

Example: Truck Purchase

Truck Purchase Price$45,000
Section 179 Deduction$45,000
Tax Bracket (estimated 25%)25%
Tax Savings$11,250
TIP: Buy your truck/trailer BEFORE December 31 of the tax year you want the deduction. Buy in November 2026 → Deduct in 2026 taxes.

Option 2: Standard Mileage Rate

Simpler method — deduct a flat rate per mile driven for business.

2026 Rate (estimated)~$0.67 per mile
Example: 20,000 business miles$13,400 deduction
IMPORTANT: You must choose ONE method and stick with it for that vehicle. Actual expenses is usually better for trucks used heavily for business.

HOME OFFICE DEDUCTION

If you run your business from home:

MethodHow It Works

RETIREMENT CONTRIBUTIONS

Reduce your taxable income by contributing to retirement:

Account Type2026 Contribution Limit
SEP IRAUp to 25% of net self-employment income (max ~$69,000)
Solo 401(k)Up to ~$69,000 (employee + employer contributions)
Traditional IRA$7,000 ($8,000 if 50+)
POWERFUL: A SEP IRA lets you contribute up to 25% of your profits — reducing your taxable income significantly.

QUARTERLY ESTIMATED TAXES

As a self-employed contractor, you must pay estimated taxes quarterly:

QuarterDue Date
Q1 (Jan-Mar)April 15
Q2 (Apr-May)June 15
Q3 (Jun-Aug)September 15
Q4 (Sep-Dec)January 15 (next year)
WARNING: If you don't pay quarterly estimates and owe more than $1,000 at tax time, you'll face penalties.
