## RECOMMENDATIONS

1. **Get your own LLC** — Protects you personally, even if taking over existing company

2. **Written indemnification** — Partner MUST agree in writing that you're not responsible for anything before the agreement date

3. **Define expenses clearly** — List exactly what counts as an expense before the profit split

4. **Cap the 10-year payout** — Consider a maximum total (e.g., $X over 10 years)

5. **Quarterly reviews** — Build in regular partnership check-ins during Phase 1

6. **Everything in writing** — If it's not written down, it doesn't exist

7. **Separate your assets** — Truck/trailer in your name, not the company's
