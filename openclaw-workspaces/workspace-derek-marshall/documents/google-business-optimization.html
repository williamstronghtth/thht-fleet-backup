REVIEWS = RANKINGS + TRUST
More reviews + higher rating = you show up first. Period.

How to Get Reviews

☐ Ask at the end of EVERY job: "Would you mind leaving us a Google review?"
☐ Send a follow-up text with direct link to review
☐ Make it easy — send the link, don't make them search
☐ Add review link to email signature
☐ Add QR code to business card linking to review page

Your Review Link

Get your direct review link:

  1. Go to your GBP dashboard
  2. Click "Get more reviews"
  3. Copy the short link
  4. Save it — use it everywhere
TEXT TEMPLATE:

"Hi [Name], thank you for choosing Affordable Roofing! If you were happy with our work, we'd really appreciate a quick Google review. It helps us grow and helps other homeowners find reliable contractors. Here's the link: [YOUR REVIEW LINK]. Thanks again!"

Responding to Reviews

☐ Reply to EVERY review (good and bad) within 24-48 hours
☐ Keep responses professional and thankful
☐ For negative reviews: Stay calm, apologize, offer to fix, take offline
POSITIVE REVIEW RESPONSE:
"Thank you so much, [Name]! It was a pleasure working on your roof. We appreciate you trusting us with your home. If you ever need anything in the future, don't hesitate to call!"
NEGATIVE REVIEW RESPONSE:
"Hi [Name], we're sorry to hear about your experience. This isn't the standard we hold ourselves to. Please call us at 386-392-8952 so we can make this right. We take every concern seriously."
