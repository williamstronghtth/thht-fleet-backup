QUESTIONS TO ASK PARTNER

  1. Are there ANY existing debts, liens, or legal issues?
  2. Are there any ongoing contracts or warranties from past jobs?
  3. Who currently carries insurance and what does it cover?
  4. Are you willing to sign a statement that I inherit no prior liability?
  5. What happens if you can't do inspections (sick, traveling)?
  6. Do you expect any role after the 10-year payout period?

RECOMMENDATIONS

  1. Get your own LLC — Protects you personally
  2. Written indemnification — Partner MUST agree in writing that you're not responsible for anything before agreement date
  3. Define expenses clearly — List exactly what counts
  4. Cap the 10-year payout — Consider a maximum total amount
  5. Quarterly reviews — Build in regular partnership check-ins
  6. Everything in writing — If it's not written, it doesn't exist
  7. Separate your assets — Truck/trailer in your name
