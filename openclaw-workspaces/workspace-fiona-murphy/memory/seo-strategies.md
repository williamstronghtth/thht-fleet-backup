## 7 High-Impact SEO Tactics

### 1. Competition Gap Analysis
Scan competitor sites and identify what they're missing. Find content gaps and cover topics that provide MORE value than them.
- **Key question:** "What are competitors missing that I can do better?"
- If you provide more value, you win the #1 spot.

### 2. Schema Audit
Check page source for existing schema markup:
- Does LocalBusiness schema exist? Is it useful?
- Identify missing/weak schema and prioritize
- Generate clean JSON-LD for HIGH priority items
- Focus on: LocalBusiness, RealEstateAgent, Service, FAQPage

### 3. Low-Hanging Fruit Keywords
Target high-intent local keywords that indicate buyer is ready NOW:
- "near me" keywords
- "emergency" keywords  
- Location + service combinations
- Example: "homes for sale Port Orange FL", "realtor near me Volusia County"

### 4. Business & Competitor Deep Dive
Extract and compare:
- **Our site:** Business name, address, services, cities served, key selling points
- **Competitors:** Services, target locations, strengths, trust signals
- Use this to identify our unique advantages

### 5. Google Business Profile (GBP) Domination
Create high-impact GBP posts with:
- Local landmarks and references
- Hard "Call Now" CTAs
- Keywords competitors aren't using
- Regular posting frequency

### 6. GBP Posting Plan
Analyze competitor GBP posts for:
- Post types (updates, offers, events)
- Posting frequency
- Content themes
- Offers and CTAs
- Media usage (photos, videos)
- Timing patterns

Create actionable posting plan based on what correlates with strong rankings.

### 7. Keyword Research
Analyze competitor top pages:
- Target keywords
- Search volumes
- Difficulty scores
- Prioritized list for content creation
