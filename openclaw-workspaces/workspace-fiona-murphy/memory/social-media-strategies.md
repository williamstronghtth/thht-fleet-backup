# Social Media Marketing Strategies

*Shared by Chris on 2026-02-16*

These are prompt frameworks for creating high-performing Instagram/social content.

---

## 1. Pattern Break Architect
**Goal:** Create scroll-stopping content that breaks overused patterns

> Act as a senior Instagram growth strategist. Analyze my niche and identify overused content patterns. Then create 10 post ideas that break those patterns while still fitting Instagram's algorithm. Each idea must feel unexpected and scroll-stopping within the first 2 seconds.

---

## 2. Hook That Hijacks Attention
**Goal:** Write brutally strong opening hooks

> Act as a viral copywriter who studies high-retention Instagram posts. Rewrite my idea into 5 brutally strong opening hooks designed to stop scrolling instantly. Each hook must create curiosity, tension, or disbelief without using clickbait or fake claims.

---

## 3. Silent Content Multiplier
**Goal:** Create faceless/voiceless content that performs

> Act as a faceless content expert. Convert this idea into a text-only or visual-only Instagram post that requires no talking, no face, and no trends. Optimize it for saves, shares, and re-reads while keeping it simple enough to post consistently.

---

## 4. Algorithm Friendly Rewrite
**Goal:** Optimize for watch time, completion rate, and saves

> Act as an Instagram algorithm analyst. Rewrite this post to maximize watch time, completion rate, and saves. Structure the copy so each line pulls the reader to the next, creating natural momentum without sounding promotional.

---

## 5. Scroll Retention Engineer
**Goal:** Force readers to keep scrolling line-by-line

> Act as a retention specialist. Break this post into a line-by-line sequence that forces readers to keep scrolling. Each line should slightly increase curiosity or value so people feel compelled to read the entire post.

---

## 6. Repurpose Everywhere
**Goal:** Turn one post into platform-native variations

> Act as a content repurposing strategist. Turn this single Instagram post into 5 variations that feel native to Threads, Instagram, and Reels captions without changing the core idea. Each version should feel fresh, not recycled.

---

## 7. Invisible Authority Builder
**Goal:** Build trust without bragging or hype

> Act as a brand positioning expert. Rewrite my content so it subtly positions me as someone who knows what they're doing without bragging, flexing numbers, or sounding like a guru. The goal is trust, not hype.

---

## How to Use These

Apply these frameworks when:
- Drafting new posts (use #1, #2 for ideation)
- Refining captions (use #4, #5 for optimization)
- Creating variations (use #3, #6 for repurposing)
- Positioning the brand (use #7 for tone)

These complement our professional, polished brand voice — use them to add engagement without sacrificing trust.
