## Blog Post

**Title:** The Compass Syndication Deal: What It Means for Home Sellers

**Content:**

Big news is shaking up the real estate industry — and if you're thinking about selling your home, you should pay attention.

Compass, one of the largest brokerages in the country, just announced a syndication deal with Rocket Homes and Redfin. On the surface, it sounds like expanded reach. But dig deeper, and critics — including Jason Oppenheim from Selling Sunset — are calling it "weaponizing secrecy."

Here's what's happening and why it matters to you.

### The Deal

Compass is creating what some describe as an alternative to the traditional MLS (Multiple Listing Service). Instead of every listing being visible to every agent and buyer, these large brokerages would share listings among themselves first.

Think of it as a VIP room where only certain agents get early access.

### Why Critics Are Concerned

**Less exposure for sellers.** The MLS exists to give your home maximum visibility. Every agent, every buyer, every platform sees it. When listings stay inside private networks — even temporarily — you're potentially missing buyers.

**Reduced competition.** More eyeballs mean more offers. More offers mean better prices. Private networks shrink that pool.

**Transparency issues.** The real estate industry has been moving toward MORE transparency, not less. This feels like a step backward.

### The "Coming Soon" Problem

This isn't entirely new. "Coming soon" and pocket listings have been controversial for years. The idea sounds exclusive, but the data consistently shows: homes listed on MLS from Day 1 sell for more money.

Why? Simple math. More exposure = more competition = higher sale price.

### What This Means Locally

We want every buyer in the market to see your home. We want multiple offers. We want you to get top dollar.

### The Bottom Line

Big brokerages are building walled gardens. They're prioritizing their networks over your net proceeds.

We prioritize your sale.
