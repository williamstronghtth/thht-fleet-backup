## V4 Model Built! 🚀

Chris asked for improvements. Built V4 with:

### Components
1. **Net Rating** (35% weight) - baseline team strength
2. **Four Factors Matchups** (35% weight) - offense vs defense:
   - eFG% vs Opp eFG%
   - TOV% vs Opp TOV%
   - ORB% vs DRB%
   - FT Rate vs Opp FT Rate
3. **Recency** (20% weight) - last 10 games trend
4. **Rest/B2B** (10% weight) - schedule factors
5. **Injuries** - tiered by minutes:
   - 30+ mpg star: 7 pts
   - 28+ All-NBA: 5 pts
   - 24+ All-Star: 3.5 pts
   - Rotation (10-20 mpg): 0.5-1.5 pts
