# NBA Injury Research Sources

## Primary Sources (Automated)
- ESPN NBA Injuries: https://www.espn.com/nba/injuries
- NBA Official Injury Report: https://official.nba.com/nba-injury-report-2024-25-season/
- Rotowire NBA Injuries: https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/nba-lineups.php

## Secondary Sources (Manual Check)
- Beat writer X accounts (via Nitter when possible)
- Team official accounts
- Shams Charania / Woj reports

## Research Workflow
1. Pull ESPN injury page daily (before bet decision)
2. Cross-reference with official NBA injury report
3. Check for late-breaking news on game day
4. Flag any "questionable" or "game-time decision" players
