## 🔍 WILLIAM'S NOTES

**Red flag to watch:** The spec says James Hardie SIDING but aluminum/vinyl TRIM. Your father-in-law specifically called this out. Hardie siding with vinyl trim is like putting premium tires on cheap rims. The trim is what's exposed to the most weathering around windows and doors. Ask if Hardie trim (HardieTrim) is standard or available.

**NH-specific concerns not on the spec sheet:**
- **Radon** — Amherst, NH is in a high radon zone. A radon mitigation system should be standard in new construction up there, not an afterthought.
- **Ice dams** — No mention of gutters OR ice/water shield. With NH winters, this is critical. At minimum: ice and water shield membrane on first 3-6 feet of roof edge, proper attic ventilation (they do have ridge/soffit vents ✅), and gutters.
- **Well water** — Private well with no mention of water treatment. NH well water commonly has arsenic, manganese, and hardness issues. A purification + softener system should be discussed.

**What looks good:**
- 2x6 exterior framing (above minimum)
- R-24 blown-in with vapor barrier (solid)
- JennAir appliances (premium)
- 200 Amp service (future-proof)
- Hardwood flooring + hardwood stairs
- Insulated fiberglass doors
- Gas fireplace standard
