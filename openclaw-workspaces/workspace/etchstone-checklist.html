🔍 ADDITIONAL NOTES
⚠️ Exterior Trim Mismatch: Spec lists James Hardie SIDING but aluminum/vinyl TRIM around windows and doors. This is where builders cut costs on an otherwise premium exterior. Vinyl trim is less durable, can warp, and doesn't match Hardie's longevity. Ask about HardieTrim (fiber cement trim) as standard or upgrade.
⚠️ NH-Specific Concerns:
• Radon: Amherst, NH is in a high-radon zone. A radon mitigation system (sub-slab depressurization) should be standard in new construction — not an afterthought or add-on.
• Ice Dams: No mention of gutters or ice & water shield membrane. NH winters demand: ice/water shield on first 3-6 feet of roof edge + properly sized gutters. Ridge/soffit vents are confirmed (good), which helps.
• Well Water: NH wells commonly have arsenic, manganese, iron, and hardness issues. A water test + purification/softener system should be discussed before closing.
