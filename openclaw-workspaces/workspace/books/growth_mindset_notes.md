## Core Concept: Fixed vs Growth Mindset

The foundation (building on Carol Dweck's research):

| Fixed Mindset | Growth Mindset |
|---------------|----------------|
| Intelligence/talent is static | Intelligence/talent can be developed |
| Avoids challenges | Embraces challenges |
| Gives up easily | Persists through obstacles |
| Sees effort as pointless | Sees effort as the path to mastery |
| Ignores criticism | Learns from criticism |
| Threatened by others' success | Inspired by others' success |

---

## Key Principles

### 1. **Embrace Challenges**
- Challenges are opportunities for growth, not threats to ego
- Comfort zone = stagnation zone
- "Not yet" is more powerful than "I can't"

### 2. **Persistence Over Talent**
- Effort beats innate ability in the long run
- Grit and determination are learnable skills
- Failure is feedback, not a final verdict

### 3. **Learn From Criticism**
- Feedback is data, not an attack
- Separate your identity from your performance
- Ask "What can I learn?" not "Why are they wrong?"

### 4. **Find Inspiration in Others' Success**
- Other people's wins prove what's possible
- Study successful people — reverse engineer their path
- Collaboration > competition

### 5. **Focus on Process, Not Outcomes**
- Celebrate effort and strategy, not just results
- Small daily improvements compound
- The journey shapes who you become

### 6. **Reframe "Failure"**
- Failure = First Attempt In Learning
- Every master was once a disaster
- Post-mortems are for learning, not blame

## Application to Real Estate Business

**For Chris (and me):**
- Cold calls that don't convert → learning opportunities
- Lost deals → what could we do differently?
- New market challenges → chance to develop new skills
- Competitor success → study their approach, adapt what works
- Client objections → feedback on our pitch/value prop

**Daily Practice:**
- End each day asking: "What did I learn today?"
- When facing a tough challenge: "This is helping me grow"
- When making mistakes: "What's the lesson here?"
