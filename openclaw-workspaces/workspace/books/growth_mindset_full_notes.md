## Core Frameworks

### 1. The Big Five Personality Model (OCEAN)
- **O**penness to Experience — adventurous, creative, broad interests
- **C**onscientiousness — organized, goal-directed, detail-oriented
- **E**xtraversion — energized by others, talkative, team-oriented
- **A**greeableness — kind, trusting, cooperative
- **N**euroticism — prone to mood swings, anxiety, emotional instability

**Key insight:** Match your growth methods to your personality type. Don't force an introvert into public speaking boot camp.

### 2. Three Levels of Growth Goals
1. **Inspirational:** "I believe I can..." (foundation of hope/encouragement)
2. **Motivational:** "When I achieve X, I will reward myself with Y" (with intrinsic rewards)
3. **Aspirational:** Detailed, far-reaching vision of success

**Formula:** You need to be INSPIRED to MOTIVATE yourself to be ASPIRATIONAL.

### 3. Helen's Pyramid of Security/Success
From bottom to top:
1. Entry level positions (learn from the bottom up)
2. Saving for retirement (never too early)
3. Your 'A' Team (co-pilots, collaborators, mentors)
4. Self-Care (critical at the top — schedule it!)
5. #1 position

### 4. Emotional Intelligence Positions (from their previous book)
- **EU** (Emotionally Unavailable) — no idea how you or others feel
- **EE** (Emotional Enlightenment) — ideal state, emotions available and interconnected
- **ee** (Emotional Empath) — obsessed with others' feelings, depleted
- **II** (Id Intelligent) — "it's all about me" but still feels empty

## Major Takeaways

### Finding Your Tribe
- Growth is impossible in a vacuum
- Tribes lower rejection risk (collective setback vs personal affront)
- Strength of many — anything feels possible when surrounded by like-minded people
- Changing perception from within (12 Angry Men example)

### Collaborate > Network
- Networking = collecting business cards like trading cards
- Collaboration = walking the talk, mutual benefit, actionable
- Fight FOR your enemies, not against them — you smell like a rose

### The Boss Types (Helen's Profiles)
| Type | Quick Win | Mortal Mistake |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| **King on the Hill** | Be the person with answers when needed | Try to joke with them |
| **Best Friend Boss** | Appreciate without obvious ass-kissing | Be funnier than them; divulge too much |
| **Explosive Boss** | Fly under radar, pray for demise | Confront them |
| **Lazy Boss** | Make sure EVERYONE knows YOU did it | Be masochistic/OCD (they'll work you to death) |
| **Started from Bottom** | Show respect for their journey | Ridicule "how things used to be" |
| **Mentor Boss** | Take challenges, deliver results, pay it forward | Let them catch you shaking someone off the ladder |

### Comfort Zone Reframe
Don't "leave your comfort zone" — leave your DISCOMFORT zone.

Most people aren't comfortable; they're locked in dysfunction they've gotten used to. Real growth = finding a genuine comfort zone of security, happiness, and control over your destiny.

### Passion + Flow
- Identify what makes you passionately alive (good AND bad, happy AND sad)
- **Flow** = "the zone" where you're fully immersed, totally invested, pure enjoyment
- If there's no passion in your work... dust off your resume
- Don't chase happiness — run toward what MAKES you happy

### Resilience Formula
**Presence + Possibility = Resilience**
- Presence = understanding how you feel in the moment
- Possibility = recognizing opportunity for growth
- Resilience = power to fight back and bounce back

### Resisting Self-Sabotage
- Stop negative self-talk: "I CAN, I WILL, I AM"
- Blame is only useful if it changes something
- You made the best choice with the info you had at the time
- Learn your lesson ONCE, then move on

### Giving
- When you're at your lowest, giving to others shifts focus and affirms your gifts
- In business: GIVE first, ask later — builds fundamental trust
- Giving without agenda creates solid relationships

### Self-Care as Priority
- Not optional, especially at the top
- Schedule it: morning yoga, lunchtime massage, evening run
- "If I don't cheer myself up every second, who will?"
