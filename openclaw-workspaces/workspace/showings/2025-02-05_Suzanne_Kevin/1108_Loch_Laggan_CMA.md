## Cash Offer Strategy

### Cash Offer Advantages
- **No financing contingency** — certainty of closing
- **Faster closing** — can close in 2-3 weeks vs 30-45 days
- **No appraisal risk** — seller doesn't worry about low appraisal
- **Typical cash discount: 3-8%** below market value

### Seller Motivation Factors
- ✅ Property listed since September 2025 (5+ months)
- ✅ Three price reductions totaling $39,900
- ✅ Listing expired and had to relist
- ✅ Carrying costs add up (HOA, insurance, taxes, maintenance)
- ✅ Seller originally paid $450,000 (2020) — still profitable at any offer above that

### Recommended Offer Range

| Tier | Offer | Rationale |
|------|-------|-----------|
| **Aggressive** | $600,000 – $615,000 | Below RVM, factors in full repair costs + cash discount. Strong opening position. |
| **Competitive** | $620,000 – $640,000 | At or near RVM, modest cash discount. Likely to generate a counter-offer. |
| **Strong** | $645,000 – $660,000 | Near top of RVM range, minimal cash discount. Most likely to be accepted quickly. |

### My Recommendation
**Start at $615,000 – $625,000.** Here's why:

1. RPR values the home at $631K — not $685K
2. No documentation of major system replacements in 35 years is a significant risk
3. Cash removes financing/appraisal uncertainty (worth ~5% to seller)
4. Seller has been unable to sell for 5 months — they need movement
5. Leaves room to negotiate up to ~$640K-$650K where both sides feel good

**If seller counters:** Be prepared to come up to $640,000-$650,000, which is still below list but within the fair value range. Require a home inspection with emphasis on roof, HVAC, plumbing, and electrical given the age and lack of permit history.
