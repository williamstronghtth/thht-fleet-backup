# New Construction Builder Evaluation Checklist

## 🏗️ Structural / Framing
- [ ] Exterior wall framing specs (2x4 vs 2x6)?
- [ ] Interior wall framing specs?
- [ ] 1st floor ceiling height?
- [ ] 2nd floor ceiling height?
- [ ] 2nd floor joist size?
- [ ] Main floor support joists — type and size (solid lumber, LVL, I-Joist, steel, or open web trusses)?

## 🔥 HVAC
- [ ] Brand and model of units?
- [ ] Heat source (heat pump, hybrid heat pump, oil, natural gas, propane, other)?
- [ ] Ductwork type (standard or small/high velocity)?
- [ ] Zone control — manual duct dampers + registers OR automatic zone dampers with room thermostats?
- [ ] Confirm: every room has both supply AND return vents?

## 🧱 Insulation & Envelope
- [ ] Exterior wall insulation method — thermal AND vapor penetration?
- [ ] Roof insulation type (blown, fiberglass batts, or spray foam)?
- [ ] Sound insulation between 1st and 2nd floors?

## 🪟 Windows
- [ ] Brand?
- [ ] U-Factor (target: 0.30 or lower)
- [ ] SHGC Factor (target: 0.25 or lower)
- [ ] VT Factor (target: 0.50 or higher)
- [ ] Low-E coating rating?
- [ ] AL rating (air leakage)?
- [ ] CR rating (condensation resistance)?

## 🏠 Exterior
- [ ] Siding material confirmed (Hardie Board)?
- [ ] ALL exterior trim, window framing, and door framing also Hardie Board or equivalent?
- [ ] Roofing material and warranty?
- [ ] Gutters included?
- [ ] Ice dam prevention provisions?

## 🚿 Plumbing
- [ ] Pipe material (copper, CPVC, PEX)?
- [ ] Kitchen fixture brand and model (Kohler, Delta, Moen, etc.)?
- [ ] Bathroom fixture brand and model?
- [ ] If well water: purification and softener system included?

## 🍳 Appliances
- [ ] Brands and specific models included?
- [ ] ENERGY STAR rated?

## 🏡 Interior Finishes
- [ ] Flooring type, brand, and grade?
- [ ] 2nd floor flooring underlayment (cork or similar)?
- [ ] Interior doors — hollow core or solid?

## 🔌 Wiring & Communication
- [ ] CAT cable with 8-pin (RJ-45) jacks?
- [ ] Wiring pattern — series or star (home run to central box)?
- [ ] Which rooms are pre-wired (all or select)?

## ⚠️ Safety & Systems
- [ ] Radon gas meter included?
- [ ] Radon evacuation exhaust system included?
- [ ] Whole house generator included or available?

## 💰 Terms & Contract
- [ ] Payment structure — construction loan with draws based on phase completion?
- [ ] Guaranteed price (adjusted only via authorized change orders)?
- [ ] Guaranteed completion/ready date?
- [ ] Penalty clause if builder misses the ready date?
