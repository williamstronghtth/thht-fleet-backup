# Cold Call Roleplay Practice — 2026-03-19

Practicing realistic prospecting call flows using objection handling scripts. Each scenario chains multiple objections like a real call would.

---

## Scenario 1: Expired Listing in Cape Coral

**Context**: Home was listed 6 months ago at $389K, didn't sell. Currently off-market.

> **Chris**: Hi, is this Margaret? Hey Margaret, this is Chris Hoover with The Hoover Home Team. I noticed your home on SE 15th Terrace was on the market earlier this year — I'm sure that was frustrating. I'm calling because I work with a lot of sellers in Cape Coral and wanted to see if selling is still on your radar?

> **Margaret**: *Yeah, we tried. Our agent didn't do much. But honestly I'm not interested right now.*

> **Chris**: Totally fair — and I'm sorry that was your experience. That's actually pretty common with expired listings. Can I share one quick thing? Cape Coral buyer activity is actually up 29% year over year right now, but inventory is down 18%. That's a very different market than 6 months ago. If you *were* to reconsider, would it help to know what your home would realistically sell for in today's market?

> **Margaret**: *I mean... I'd be curious. But I'd need to talk to my husband first before doing anything.*

> **Chris**: Of course — big decision. How about this: I'll put together a quick market analysis specific to your home — no obligation, no pressure. That way you and your husband can look at real numbers together and decide if it even makes sense. Would email work, or would you prefer I drop off a printed copy?

> **Margaret**: *Email's fine. It's margaret.wilson@gmail.com.*

> **Chris**: Perfect, I'll have that to you by tomorrow afternoon. And Margaret — if you have any questions when you're looking it over, don't hesitate to call me back. Have a great evening.

**Result**: Got email, sending CMA, established rapport. Follow up in 3 days.

## Scenario 2: FSBO in Jacksonville

**Context**: Home listed on Zillow as For Sale By Owner, $325K, been up 45 days.

> **Chris**: Hey David, this is Chris Hoover — I'm a local agent here in Jacksonville. I saw your home on Oakwood Drive is for sale. Looks like a great property. How's it going so far?

> **David**: *It's going fine. We've had some showings. We're handling it ourselves.*

> **Chris**: Respect that — honestly, you clearly know your home's value to put it out there. The showings are a good sign too. Quick question — are the people coming through pre-approved, or are you getting a mix?

> **David**: *Uh, I haven't really asked that. Mostly people from Zillow.*

> **Chris**: That's pretty normal for FSBOs. Here's what I'd flag — probably half of Zillow inquiries aren't qualified buyers. They're lookers. The risk is you spend weeks showing to people who can't actually close. One thing I offer FSBO sellers — totally free — is I'll run a comp analysis for you and give you a buyer qualification checklist you can use at the door. No strings. Would that be helpful?

> **David**: *I mean... sure, that can't hurt. But I'm not paying 6% commission.*

> **Chris**: I hear you. And I'm not asking you to commit to anything. Let me send you the comps and the checklist, and if down the road you want to have a conversation about what working together could look like — and what the net difference actually is — we can cross that bridge then. Fair?

> **David**: *Yeah, that works.*

**Result**: Sending CMA + buyer qual checklist. Planted the seed on commission/net value. Follow up in 1 week.

## Scenario 3: Sphere of Influence Check-In

**Context**: Past client who bought 3 years ago. Haven't spoken in a while.

> **Chris**: Hey Tom! Chris Hoover here — how are you guys doing? How's the house on Maple treating you?

> **Tom**: *Oh hey Chris! Yeah, it's great. We actually just redid the kitchen.*

> **Chris**: No way — that's awesome. Kitchens are huge for value. You know, between the kitchen and the way your area's trending, your equity has probably jumped a lot since you bought. Want me to pull a quick update on what it's worth now? Just good to know even if you're staying put.

> **Tom**: *Sure, that'd be cool. We're not looking to move though.*

> **Chris**: Totally — think of it as a financial checkup. I'll send it over this week. Hey, one more thing — do you know anyone in your neighborhood or at work who's been talking about buying or selling? I'm always looking to help good people like you guys.

> **Tom**: *Actually... my coworker Mark was just saying he wants to sell his condo. Let me get you his number.*

**Result**: Referral generated. This is why sphere touches matter.

## Key Takeaways from Practice

1. **Chain multiple objections** — Real calls don't stop at one "no." Scripts need to flow naturally from one objection to the next.
2. **Market stats are the Swiss Army knife** — Every objection can be redirected with one relevant data point. The market snapshot script I built earlier feeds directly into this.
3. **The free CMA is the universal door-opener** — Works for expired, FSBO, sphere, cold calls... everyone wants to know their home value.
4. **Ask about their experience, not their objection** — "How's it going?" and "Are buyers pre-approved?" are more powerful than trying to overcome resistance.
5. **Referral ask should be standard** — Every sphere call should end with "do you know anyone who..."
