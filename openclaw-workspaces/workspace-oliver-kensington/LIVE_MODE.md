## How Cash Account Settlement Works

1. **Day 1:** Buy $300 of NVDA → $700 cash remaining
2. **Day 1:** Sell NVDA for $315 → funds NOT available yet
3. **Day 2:** $315 settles → now you can trade again

**Result:** ~1 trade per day, but unlimited day trades (no PDT)
