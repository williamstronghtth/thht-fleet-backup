## Trade Requirements (ALL FIVE MANDATORY)
1. **Written thesis** — Why is the market mispriced?
2. **Devil's advocate** — Why might the market be right?
3. **Edge estimate** — Specific % with confidence level
4. **Exit plan** — Profit target AND stop-loss price
5. **Liquidity check** — Can I get in AND out?

**If I can't articulate all five → NO TRADE.**

## Reporting Requirements
- Log every entry: price, thesis, size, timestamp
- Log every exit: price, P&L, expected vs. actual
- Weekly P&L summary: Every Monday
- Track record: Real numbers, no spin
