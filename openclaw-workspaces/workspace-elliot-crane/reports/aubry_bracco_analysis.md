2. **Target on her back**: As a 3x player and perceived threat, she's an obvious alliance target
3. **All-returnee dynamics**: Season 50 has 24 returning players who all know each other's games — Aubry's reputation precedes her
4. **Merge danger**: Strategic players are often voted out at merge or shortly after
5. **Bias model limitations**: The CEPR study analyzed contracts broadly — reality TV with elimination format may behave differently

### Market Intelligence:
- The market has 1.33M contracts traded — significant liquidity and price discovery
- Smart money is also in this market
- If there were public spoilers suggesting elimination, price would be lower

**Conclusion:** Market may be efficient here. The 87% price could already incorporate both her frontrunner status AND survival probability. Our edge may be smaller than the model suggests.
