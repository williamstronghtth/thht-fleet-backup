# Morning Brief — July 15, 2026

## Mortgage Rates 🏠
**Current Status:** 30-year fixed at **6.771%** (highest levels of the year)
- 15-year fixed: 5.776%
- Trend: Rates have risen throughout early July, driven by Middle East conflict (Iran war since late February) pushing oil/inflation higher
- Fed Outlook: Majority of policymakers expect rate hike later this year, not a cut; inflation still well above 2% target
- Expert Consensus: 60% expect rates to rise in coming days; 20% expect decrease; 20% expect flat

## Real Estate Market — National 📊
**Existing Home Sales:** Down 2.4% (June vs. May), but UP 2.8% year-over-year
**Inventory Growth:** Slower than expected — Realtor.com revised forecast to 3.6% supply growth by end of 2026 (down from 8.9% forecast in December)
**Year-End Rate Forecast:** Realtor.com expects 30-year mortgage at 6.3% by end of 2026

**Regional Highlights:**
- San Diego: June sales ↑9.5% YoY; median price $950K (↑4.4% YoY)
- Houston: Luxury segment (+$1M) ↑10.1% YoY; 481 transactions closed in May

**Industry News:** 21st Century ROAD to Housing Act now law; CMLS named Jessica Edgerton as new CEO (effective July 1)

## Southern NH / Hillsborough County Market 🔑
**Median Sale Price:** $548,453 (June 2026) — **↑4.5% YoY**
**Days to Sell:** 23 days median
**Above-Ask Performance:** 61% of homes sold above list price; typical home goes 2% above asking

**Inventory Status:**
- Active listings: 1,482 (↑11% YoY) — highest June total since 2021
- Months of supply: 1.6 (still well below 4-6 months for balance)
- 16 consecutive months of YoY inventory growth
- Homes taking longer to sell than a year ago, but still intense seller's market

**Market Outlook:** Strong seller's market with signs of gradual rebalancing; Hillsborough remains most competitive/expensive corridor (alongside Rockingham & Seacoast)
