## "The Real Commute" — a phone-and-drone YouTube/Shorts format that answers the ONE question every MA-to-NH buyer secretly googles

**What I noticed:** Our best NH content so far is guessing games and calendar hooks — engagement plays. But the deepest relocation anxiety for a family moving up from Massachusetts isn't "which town is cute." It's *"if we move to the Valley, how bad is the drive — to Boston, to my office, to the airport — really?"* Nobody can answer that from Zillow. Chris can, because he lives here. That's a content moat no competitor in this market can copy.

**The idea:** A recurring, dirt-cheap video series where Chris films **one real commute, in real rush-hour traffic, with a live timer on screen** — and answers the question a remote buyer physically cannot test themselves.

- Timer counts up in the corner (**cobalt #2563A9**), a sunflower **"FINISH"** stamp hits at the destination, lower-third names the exact route + time of day.
- The hook is a built-in curiosity gap: *"Amherst to the Boston line at 8am. Real traffic. Let's see the final number."* People watch to the end for the reveal — same tension as a stopwatch reveal in a speedrun or a closing-line in a betting card.
- Counterprograms every glossy listing tour in the feed by filming the *boring-but-decisive* thing. That's exactly why it stands out.

**First 3 episodes (real Valley routes, real questions):**
1. **Amherst → the MA line toward Boston, 8:00am** — "Can we still keep our Boston-area jobs?" THE question.
2. **Mont Vernon → Manchester (Rte 13→101), 8:00am** — city + airport access from the quiet town.
3. **Milford → Nashua (Rte 101A), 8:00am** — the everyday errands/employment hub run.

**Why it's strong (ties to a real outcome):**
- **On-mission:** feeds the new YouTube channel directly, and every episode is an SEO magnet for a *town name + "commute"* search — exactly the from-scratch visibility the NH launch needs.
- **Un-copyable:** requires being physically on the ground here. Proof-of-local-knowledge in 60 seconds.
- **Cheap + repeatable:** phone mount or DJI, no batch shoot, no image-inventory problem (Fiona's #1 constraint — this *starves* it).
- **Repurposes five ways:** each clip embeds on the matching town page (Ryan's `thht-communities` — huge UX/SEO win), cuts to a Short (Fiona), a newsletter "commute of the week" (you), and a Jack email P.S. ("Curious how far Boston really is? I drove it — 2-min video →").

**Lead:** Chris shoots (Canon/DJI kit he already runs) → Fiona packages titles/thumbnails/distribution. Loop Ryan for the town-page embed slot once episode 1 proves the format.

**Guardrail:** use honest, unedited drive times — if a commute is long, say so (honesty *is* the brand; a real 55-min number is more trustworthy than a suspiciously clean one). Public roads only, no filming private homes.
