## The idea
A recurring **YouTube long-form** format where Chris **drafts the Souhegan Valley** like a fantasy draft. Round by round, on the clock, he picks towns *and* neighborhoods against a specific need and says *why he'd take it there*:

- **Round 1 — Best value pick** (most house/land per dollar right now)
- **Round 2 — Best sleeper** (overlooked, punching above its price)
- **Round 3 — Best for families** (schools, sidewalks, Saturday-morning stuff)
- **Round 4 — Best for the commuter** (the real Nashua/128 math)
- **Round 5 — Highest ceiling** (where he'd bet on appreciation)
- **Round 6 — Safest floor** (holds value when the market wobbles)

Each pick = ~60–90 sec of Chris's actual reasoning. Ends with his full "big board." A branded **draft-card graphic** (cobalt/sunflower, ribbon motif) stamps each pick — instantly ownable, and each card is a free-standing Short/reel for Fiona.

## Why it's strong
- **It's argument-bait, and that's the point.** Nobody who lives here can watch Chris "draft" their town in Round 4 without commenting. Locals defending Milford vs. Hollis in the comments = free reach + the algorithm's favorite fuel. Engagement is the mechanic, not a hope.
- **It proves deep local knowledge from scratch** — the exact trust a new-market agent has to earn. You can't fake a good draft board; it *shows* he knows the Valley cold.
- **Serves the #1 content priority (YouTube).** This is a native long-form format with a Pozek-style bingeable hook, not another one-off Short. It also spawns 6+ Shorts per episode with zero extra shoots.
- **Fresh seam.** Cross-pollinated from Eno's fantasy-baseball draft vocabulary (value pick, sleeper, ceiling/floor, big board) — a corner of Chris's world I've never borrowed from, and one Chris personally lives in (fantasy baseball + IDP football).

## Roles
- **Lead:** William (script the round structure + Chris's reasoning beats) + Chris (the takes — this only works in *his* voice).
- **Fiona:** titles/thumbnail ("I Drafted Every Town in the Souhegan Valley"), the draft-card template, and slicing each pick into a Short.
- **Ryan:** a live "Valley Draft Board" on the thht-communities town pages — Chris's rankings by category, evergreen SEO ("best value town Souhegan Valley").
- **Eno:** the actual cross-poll gift — sanity-check the draft *framing* so the fantasy vocabulary lands authentically (round logic, tiers, "reach" vs. "steal").
- **Jack:** each round is a subject line ("Why I'd draft Mont Vernon in Round 2").

## Guardrail
Frame every pick as Chris's **opinion / market-level** call (value, lifestyle fit, appreciation outlook) — same discipline as "The Line." Never disparage a town or value a competitor's specific listing. It's entertainment built on real knowledge, not a ranking that names losers.
