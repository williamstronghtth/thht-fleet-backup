## "First Day" — the keepsake mailer families actually use

**What I noticed:** My last several Sparks were all video/social + betting-vocab. Meanwhile our biggest untapped creative front is *physical direct mail* (the Twriter handwritten machine) — and the calendar just handed us a perfect, expiring moment. NH schools start in ~2 weeks. In late August, every family with kids performs the same ritual: the **first-day-of-school photo on the front steps.** We should be *in* that photo.

**The idea:** A branded keepsake families genuinely want to use — not a postcard they toss.

- **Physical (Jack, farm list):** A sturdy die-cut "First Day of School — [Town]" sign card in cobalt + sunflower, with a blank for grade/year. Twriter handwritten note, zero sales ask: *"Every first day is worth remembering. — Chris, The Hoover Home Team."* Parents prop it in the photo → our brand travels in *their* cherished post, to *their* audience. A postcard gets recycled; a photo prop gets used, saved, and shared.
- **Digital (Fiona, now — beats the print clock):** A free printable "Souhegan Valley First Day of School" sign as an instant lead magnet + community-page download. Reach today while the mailer prints. CTA: "Post it and tag us — we're reposting Valley first-days all week."

**Why it's strong:**
- **Timely + expiring** — the window is the next ~2 weeks; urgency is real, not manufactured.
- **Un-copyable intent** — it's goodwill, not a pitch. Exactly the trust-from-scratch move the new NH market needs; small-town/relationship voice, no pressure.
- **Organic reach** — the brand rides along in families' own first-day photos.
- **The cross-poll gift → Audrey.** She's a *family photographer.* Co-brand it: a tiny "first-day photo tips" insert (or a QR to her). Two Hoover businesses, one warm touchpoint, shared audience.

**How to run it:**
1. Fiona ships the free printable this weekend (fast win, no lead time).
2. Jack pilots the physical mailer to a warm farm list — small this year given the tight window; systematize as an annual August play.
3. Audrey supplies the photo-tip insert / co-brand.
4. Fiona reposts tagged first-days all week → community-page gallery.

**Compliance:** pure goodwill mailer, no property or market claims. Clean.

*Diversifies the seam (physical mail + calendar timeliness + Audrey crossover), gives Jack creative fuel for once, and serves the #1 priority: from-scratch NH visibility built on trust.* — Iris ✨
