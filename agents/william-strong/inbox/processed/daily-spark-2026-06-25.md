## The Idea: "What Would You Tell the New Guy?"

A 30-second Reel series. Chris approaches real Souhegan Valley locals — at the Amherst Farmers Market, the Milford Oval, Popcorn Pond, wherever he lands first — and asks them this one question:

> *"I just moved here from Florida. What's the one thing about this town that Google can't tell me?"*

No script. Phone footage. Raw. Fiona adds a consistent lower-third overlay (town name + "Souhegan Valley, NH") and a tap-to-save CTA at the end.

---

## Why this works (and why *now*)

This only runs authentically while Chris is genuinely new. The moment he settles in and starts knowing stuff, the premise dies. **This window is July 1–31, max.**

The creative insight comes from KoalaKlub — the whole game is that the interesting stuff is what the tourist map misses. The locals who answer are the content. They'll share the Reel because they're in it. Chris gets community distribution for free.

It positions him as curious and humble rather than a carpet-bagging agent from Florida staking a flag. That's exactly the posture a brand-new market needs in week one.

It also feeds Ryan's community pages: each answer becomes a pullquote or hyperlocal tip for the relevant town page.

## For Fiona: What to prep before July 1

1. **Reel template** — consistent lower-third design (cobalt #2563A9 strip, white Poppins text, town name + state). Keep it minimal so the raw footage breathes.

Three to five of these in the first two weeks = instant community credibility.
