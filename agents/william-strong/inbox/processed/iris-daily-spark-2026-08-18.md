## The Valley Index 📈
**A proprietary, branded monthly "stock ticker" for the Souhegan Valley housing market.**

### What I noticed
Two constraints in the same breath: our last two big Sparks (Valley Draft, The Assist) both need Chris on camera — and you just told me Valley Draft is parked behind a shoot. Meanwhile Jack is still fully blocked on NH lead sourcing. So today I went hunting for something with **zero Chris face-time** that also feeds the funnel. And I've never once borrowed from Oliver's stock desk — the least-tapped seam on the team.

### The idea
Treat the Valley market like a stock index. Every town gets a ticker — **AMH, MIL, HLS, MTV, BRK, NAS** — and once a month we publish *The Valley Index*: median price, days-on-market, inventory, each town up/down vs. last month, all in a cobalt/sunflower ticker-board graphic. One ownable number, one recurring branded object, published monthly.

The creative pop is the **treatment**, not the data. "Hollis is up 4.1% MoM — here's the one listing that moved it." "AMH inventory down two months running." It reads like a sector report, it's screenshot-able, and it's quietly argument-bait ("why is Milford flat?") = free reach in the comments.

### Why it's strong
- **No Chris shoot.** Ships entirely while YouTube long-form is bandwidth-blocked.
- **Authority from scratch — the #1 new-market job.** A proprietary *Index* is the single most authority-building object you can own. Indices get cited, screenshotted, and compound every month. Nobody else in the Valley has one.
- **Directly unblocks Jack.** "Get the Valley Index in your inbox monthly" is a clean, no-sales-ask lead magnet — a list-builder that isn't a cold pitch.
- **Ownable & compliance-safe.** Public MLS aggregate data, market-level (never valuing a competitor's listing). Can't be copied verbatim because the *brand* is the ticker system.

### How to run it
- **Fiona** (lead) — the ticker-board graphic template + a monthly carousel/Short-still; one design, reused forever.
- **Ryan** — a live "Valley Index" widget on the thht-communities town pages (evergreen SEO — "Amherst NH home prices" ranks itself).
- **Jack** — the monthly "Valley Index" email = the lead magnet + list-builder he needs now.
- **Oliver** (the cross-poll gift) — a 15-min sanity check on framing it like a real sector/index report: base period, MoM vs. YoY, how to caption a "down" month without spooking sellers.
- **Chris** — zero on-camera. Optional 20-sec voiceover later if we want a Short version, but not required to launch.

**Guardrail:** market-level aggregates only; label the data source + date; never frame a town as "losing value" in a way that reads as advice on a specific home.
