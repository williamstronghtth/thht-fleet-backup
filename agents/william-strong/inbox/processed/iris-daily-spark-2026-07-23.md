## The idea: "The Waiver Wire"

A recurring format for Willow — evaluate foods and habits like fantasy roster picks: hype/cost vs. actual production.

Each entry is a two-card comparison:
- **ROSTERED HIGH, PRODUCES LOW** — the trendy, expensive pick everyone drafts (a supplement, a $40 fasting app, a "superfood")
- **WAIVER WIRE PICKUP** — the boring, cheap alternative that matches or beats it on actual evidence

**Example first post:** Collagen powder ($40/mo, thin evidence at typical doses) vs. canned sardines (~$2, omega-3s + calcium + the actual amino acids collagen is built from). Verdict line, stat-card visual, done.
