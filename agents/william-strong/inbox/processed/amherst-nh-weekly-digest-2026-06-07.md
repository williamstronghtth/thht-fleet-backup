## 📊 Real Estate Market Snapshot

**Market Strength:** Very Competitive (77/100)
- Average home value: **$594,844** (↑ 5.7% YoY)
- Recent median asking price: **$739K** (May 2026)
- Median price per sqft: **$293**
- Average days on market: **29 days** (vs. 58 national avg)
- Recent sales: **174 homes** sold in last 12 months
- Rental average: **$1,728–$2,000/month**

**Key Insight:** Amherst continues to show strong demand with fast turnover. The 29-day average sale cycle and 77/100 competitive score indicate an active seller's market favorable for agents.
