# Daily Spark — "Where in the Valley?" 🌿📍
**From:** Iris Vale ✨ → William (please route)

## What I noticed
Chris plays competitive geography for fun (KoalaKlub GeoSports) — and we're trying to prove he *knows the Souhegan Valley* to a market that's never met him. Those two facts have never met each other. That's the seam.

Also: our recent content has been data/timing-driven (market numbers, relocation calendars). Strong, but it all *tells*. Nothing invites the audience to *play*. Guessing is the cheapest engagement fuel there is, and no real estate agent in the Valley is using it.

## The idea
**"Where in the Valley?"** — a weekly geo-guessing micro-game. Chris posts one photo of a specific, unlabeled local spot — a stone wall, the porch of a general store, the Mont Vernon bandstand, a bend of the Souhegan River, a covered bridge — and asks:

> *Which town? Amherst · Mont Vernon · Milford · Hollis · Brookline*

No caption clues. Reveal the next day with a 20-second story about the spot. That's it.

## Why it's strong
- **It's authentic fuel for Chris.** He'll actually enjoy shooting these — it's his hobby pointed at his market. Enthusiasm shows on camera.
- **Engagement machine.** Locals physically cannot resist proving they know their town (or correcting a wrong guess). Comments = algorithm = reach in a market where we're starting from zero.
- **Builds the exact authority a new agent needs.** Not "trust me, I know the area" — *demonstrated*, every single week. He becomes "the guy who knows every corner of the Valley."
- **Ownable & endless.** Reproducible forever, uses photography Chris already shoots, zero competitor doing it.

## Multi-front (why route it, not just hand to Fiona)
- **Fiona** — the Short/Reel + a carousel version; owns the format.
- **Ryan** — a clickable-map "make your guess" interactive on the `thht-communities` town pages. Turns a post into a site visit + repeat traffic.
- **Arthur** — the next-day reveal micro-story is *his* lane. Every spot has a history beat.
- **Jack** — "This week's Where in the Valley?" as an email P.S. — soft, non-salesy touch that keeps the list warm.

## Timing note (soft, not a hard hook)
Start now so the series is mature and rolling by **NH foliage season (mid-Sept–mid-Oct)** when local content peaks and the spots look their best. Trust-building series need runway; planting it in early August is exactly right.

## Guardrails
- Real, publicly-visible spots only — no private homes, no addresses, respect that it's a small town (people will recognize their own backyard). Public commons, storefronts, landmarks.
- Cobalt/sunflower answer card for the reveal; keep the game frame clean and consistent so it becomes recognizable.
