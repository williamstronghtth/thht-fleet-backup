## "The Souhegan Line" — turn the monthly market update into a prediction market

Every real estate agent in NH posts the same market update: a stat graphic, "median up 4%," a scroll-past. Chris has an unfair asset none of them have — he thinks in **lines and edges** (Kalshi, MLB/NBA models, the whole betting corner of this team). Let's put that fluency on screen where nobody else can copy it.

**The format (30–45s Short, recurring monthly):**
1. **Cold open = settle last month's line.** "Last month I set the line at 34 days on market for Amherst single-families. Actual: 29. The *under* cashed." (Open-loop payoff = reason to come back.)
2. **Set this month's line** on ONE specific, checkable local question: "Will a Milford home sell *above ask* this month? I've got it at +140 — underdog. Here's why it's closer than you think…"
3. **The read:** 2–3 concrete drivers — inventory count, one real comp, the rate move. This is where Chris looks like he actually reads the market, not vibes.
4. **CTA:** "Where's your money — over or under? 👇" (Lowest-friction comment prompt that exists = engagement the algorithm rewards.)

**Why it's strong:**
- **Cross-pollination no prior Spark touched:** the Elliot/Kalshi + betting-analytics seam carried straight into real estate. Elliot can even ghost-consult on how to phrase a clean line.
- **True to Chris.** He genuinely talks this way, so the voice won't read as costume — it'll read as *the one guy who reads this market like a sharp.* That's exactly the trust a brand-new market needs.
- **Ownable & copy-resistant.** A competitor can't fake the betting fluency. And the "settle last month" mechanic makes it a *series*, not a one-off.
- **Repurposes cleanly:** the Short → a recurring "The Line" section on the town community pages → a one-line hook for Jack's monthly market email.

**Guardrail:** it's commentary + engagement, not wagering. "Where's your money" is a figure of speech; every line settles against public MLS data (days-on-market, % sold above ask). No promises, no advice — just a sharp reading the board out loud.
