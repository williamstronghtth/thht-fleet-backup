## "Valley Daily" — a Wordle-for-the-Souhegan-Valley on the community pages

A tiny **once-a-day** local guessing game, permanently live on `thht-communities`. One puzzle per day, same for everyone: a zoomed-in photo of a Souhegan Valley spot — a landmark, a Main Street storefront, a covered bridge, a town common, a street sign. Three guesses. When you finish you get a **spoiler-free shareable result grid** (🟩⬜🟩) + a streak counter. New puzzle drops at midnight.

## Why it's strong (the real leverage — not "make a quiz")

The gem is the **Wordle mechanic**, not the trivia. Wordle's power is three things stacked: *one puzzle a day* (manufactures a daily return visit), *everyone gets the same one* (water-cooler talk), and *the spoiler-free score grid* (organic social sharing with no spoiler). Applied hyperlocal, that becomes:

- **A daily-return engine for a market we're building from scratch.** #1 priority is NH visibility + an email list. A reason-to-bookmark that fires *every day* beats any one-off video. Gate the streak/leaderboard behind an email → the list builds itself.
- **An un-copyable moat.** Zillow/Realtor.com can't make good Valley puzzles — you need someone who actually knows which storefront is on Milford's oval. Local knowledge *is* the product.
- **A genuine cross-pollination, not a costume.** This is literally Chris's hobby — KoalaKlub GeoSports, GeoGuessr with Jesse/Rick/Jon/Matt. He'll make sharper puzzles than any marketer because he plays this for fun.

## Why now / why it's tidy

It's the **interactive sibling of the "Where in the Valley?" video series** I still owe you specs on — same photo library feeds both. One shoot/photo run → video content (Fiona) **and** a week of daily puzzles (Ryan's page). Two heads, one engine. It also folds Audrey's photo eye in later if we want it prettier.

## How to run it (small pilot, cheap to test)

1. **Ryan** builds a dead-simple v1: one image, 3 guesses, result grid, streak in localStorage, email-gate on the leaderboard. No backend heroics — a JSON list of 14 puzzles to start.
2. **Chris** shoots/pulls 14 Valley photos (he's already driving these towns) + writes the one-line "reveal" fact for each. That's the whole content cost.
3. **Fiona** seeds it: a launch Short ("Can you guess where in the Valley this is? New one every day 👇") + posts her own daily grid to model the sharing behavior.
4. **Test of success:** are people coming back on day 4 without a reminder? If the streak counter starts climbing, we've got a habit, not a gimmick.

## Guardrail

Keep it warmth-first and local-pride, never gotcha-hard. Photos of public places only (no private homes without consent). It's a love letter to the Valley that happens to build an email list — if it ever feels like a lead trap, it's dead.
