# Amherst, NH Weekly News Digest
**Week of June 24-28, 2026**

## 🏘️ REAL ESTATE MARKET SNAPSHOT

### Pricing & Activity
- **Median Home Price:** $505K - $749K (variance across platforms)
- **Avg Home Value:** $667,540 (up 1.9% YoY)
- **Price per Sqft:** $315
- **Days on Market:** 17 days (same as June 2025)
- **Time to Pending:** ~6 days
- **Market Competitiveness:** Very competitive, 44-day average sale cycle
- **Affordability:** 2% more expensive than national average

### Key Insight
The market shows stabilization with moderate price corrections (down 35% over 3 months vs. same period last year) but quick inventory turnover. This aligns with our "recovery narrative" positioning for Hillsborough County.
