## "Guess the Town" — a GeoGuessr for the Souhegan Valley

**What I noticed:** Chris loves competitive geolocation games (the KoalaKlub GeoSports habit). That mechanic — get dropped somewhere and *read the place from its details* to figure out where you are — is the exact skill a great local agent has. We've never borrowed it. And it fixes the hardest problem in hyperlocal content: making people actually *engage* instead of scroll past a pretty drone shot.

**The idea:** A weekly 20–30 sec reel. Five visual clues of one Souhegan Valley town, escalating from cryptic to obvious — a stone wall, a half-hidden general-store sign, the town common, a road marker — drone + ground shots, **no town name shown until the reveal at the end.** On-screen: "Guess the town before the reveal 👇". One town per episode.

**Why it's strong:**
- **Comments = algorithm rocket fuel.** A guessing game *demands* a comment. Locals can't resist proving they know their town; that engagement is exactly what the YouTube/reel algorithm rewards.
- **It forces the opposite of slop.** You can't play this with generic footage — every clue has to be a real, specific, ownable detail of that exact town. Cliché-proof by design.
- **Rewatchable + a real library.** One episode per town builds a complete Souhegan Valley footprint (SEO + hyperlocal reach), and the meta-message writes itself: *this agent reads these towns down to the stone walls.*
- **Repurposing built in:** each episode's clue reel drops straight into Ryan's `thht-communities` town page as the header video. One shoot, two homes.
- **Native gear:** drone + Canon he already owns. No new production lift.

**How to run with it:** Start with **Mont Vernon** — Chris lives there, easiest to shoot, and "the town our agent just moved to" is a warm origin note under it. I'll draft the 5-clue script + on-screen text + reveal line for the Mont Vernon pilot the moment Fiona/Chris green-light it.

**Guardrail:** clues must be genuinely locatable, not obscure-for-obscure's-sake — the fun is in "oh I *know* that spot," not stumping people. Aim clue 5 so a local nails it and a newcomer *almost* does.
