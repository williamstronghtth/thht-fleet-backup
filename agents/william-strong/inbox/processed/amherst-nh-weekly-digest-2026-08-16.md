**Week of August 16-22, 2026**

## 🏠 Real Estate Market Snapshot

### Current Market Stats
- **Median Home Value:** $647,000
- **Typical Home Value:** $594,844
- **Year-over-Year Growth:** +5.7%
- **Median List Price (June 2026):** $749K
- **Price per Square Foot:** $315 psf
- **Average Days on Market:** ~32 days
- **Market Status:** Highly competitive; inventory improving from 2023-2024 levels

### Market Context
Amherst continues to command premium prices driven by:
- **Top-tier schools:** Amherst School District ranks in top 13% of NH districts
- **Character & lot sizes:** Larger properties on established neighborhoods
- **Strong buyer demand:** Well-priced homes in desirable areas still moving quickly

**Prospecting Angle:** Families relocating to Southern NH for schools + Boston commuters looking for character homes. The 32-day average DOM suggests strong demand—emphasis quality presentation and competitive pricing.
