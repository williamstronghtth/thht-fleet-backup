## The idea: "First Winter"
A warm, honest newcomer's series that answers the ONE question every MA/FL-to-NH relocating buyer is silently afraid to ask: *"Can I actually handle a New England winter?"* — and it launches NOW because mid-August is when the locals' prep clock actually starts.

**The hook that makes it work:** "Here's what the people in your new town are quietly doing in August that newcomers won't think about until it's too late." Plow contracts and firewood **sell out by fall** — so the urgency is real, and the framing gives Chris insider status a transplant would never have… except Chris literally just moved to Mont Vernon July 1 and is facing his own first winter. That makes this un-fakeable and un-copyable.

## Why it's strong
- **Fresh seam:** every recent Spark was format/mechanic-driven. This one attacks the biggest UNSPOKEN emotional objection in the pipeline. Most agents dodge winter (don't want to scare buyers). Chris leans in — which IS the from-scratch NH thesis: honesty-as-edge disarms the new-market skeptic.
- **Timely + insider:** "locals are doing X *right now* in August" is time-perfect and creates urgency. A generic foliage/winter post could run anytime; this one has an expiring clock.
- **Chris's real POV:** he's living his first Mont Vernon winter this year. The camera can't fake that — it's the most authentic on-screen position he has.
- **Top-of-funnel magnet:** practical + saveable → strong lead-magnet + email-list fuel for the from-scratch market.

## How to run it (one shoot → many surfaces)
- **Chris/Fiona (lead):** flagship Short — "5 things locals do in August that newcomers miss" (book the plow guy, order your cord of wood, what mud season is, the gutter/roof-rake thing, the one grocery-day-before-a-storm habit). Warm, funny-honest, first-person-newbie.
- **Jack (lead magnet):** "The Newcomer's First-Winter Checklist" — Twriter one-pager + email opt-in. This is the download that grows the list.
- **Ryan:** a "Winter-Ready" section on each town page (local plow/firewood vendors, avg first-frost date) — hyperlocal + SEO for "moving to [town] NH winter" searches.
- **Arthur (cross-poll gift):** history beat — how Souhegan Valley towns actually survived winters (root cellars, the general store, the meetinghouse). Same week = amplification.
- **Willow (rare natural tie-in):** cold-weather immunity / winter-eating habits micro-post. First time a Spark hands Willow a seat at the table.

## Pilot
Chris films the flagship this week in Mont Vernon (his own driveway/woodpile = the set). Success test: **the comments fill with "nobody told me about the plow guy."** If newcomers feel *seen*, it's working — and every one is a warm lead.
