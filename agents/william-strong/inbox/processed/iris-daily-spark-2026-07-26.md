## "The New Neighbor's Question" — a reverse-prospecting handwritten card

**What I noticed:** Chris has an asset right now that no competing agent can copy and that expires in a few months — he is *genuinely new to town.* He moved to Mont Vernon July 1. Every other NH prospecting piece will sound like an out-of-state agent asking "thinking of selling?" Chris can say something none of them can say truthfully: *I just moved here too.* We haven't spent that anywhere yet, and the newcomer window is closing.

**The idea:** A handwritten Twriter direct-mail card that does the *opposite* of prospecting. Instead of asking for their business, Chris asks for their help:

> "Hi — I'm Chris. My family just moved to Mont Vernon this month, and I'm putting together an honest guide to the Souhegan Valley for people thinking about making the move here. You've clearly got the local knowledge I don't yet. If you had 30 seconds: what's the one thing you'd tell someone new to town? I'd genuinely love to know. — Chris
> P.S. I'm also a Realtor here now, so if you ever need one who actually knows the neighborhood, you know where to find me."

**Why it's strong:**
- **Ownable + time-limited.** Only Chris can send this, and only for a few more months. Urgency to *use it* is real, not manufactured.
- **It's relationship-first, which is exactly the NH play.** It opens a two-way conversation instead of a pitch. Replies = warm relationships in a market being built from zero.
- **The handwritten channel finally has a reason to be handwritten.** A real question from a real new neighbor *demands* the Twriter feel. A pitch never did.
- **Cross-pollination:** it borrows the "scouting report" instinct from Chris's fantasy/betting world — Chris is literally scouting his own new town and sharing findings. New texture, not another reel.
