## The Idea: "Halftime Report" — Mid-Year Souhegan Valley Market Video

It's MLB All-Star Break week. That's when the best analysts do their sharpest work: mid-season grades, the surprises, the second-half preview. Nobody in real estate borrows that format — and Chris should own it first.

**The concept:** A 6–8 minute YouTube video structured like a genuine mid-season breakdown:

1. **First-half grades** — What was predicted for Souhegan Valley in January vs. what actually happened: days on market, list-to-sale ratio, price movement, which towns surprised him
2. **The unexpected play** — The one data point or neighborhood nobody saw coming
3. **Second-half preview** — What Chris is watching, what he thinks happens next

**Opening line:**
> *"We're at halftime. Here's where the Souhegan Valley market actually stands — and what nobody's telling you about the second half."*

**Why this is the move:**
Normal market-update videos feel like homework. "Halftime Report" feels like something you'd actually want to watch — it borrows the urgency of sports analysis (the season's half over, have you been paying attention?) and the grades format generates opinions, which drive comments and shares. More importantly: it puts Chris in the analyst seat, not the agent seat. That's the trust-building positioning the NH launch needs.
