## The Spark: "The Scoreboard" — a buying-window countdown for NH family buyers

**What I noticed:** August is 8 days out. School district is the #1 reason families choose a town in the Souhegan Valley — and right now we're inside the exact, narrow window where a family that wants their kid on the first-day bus in Amherst/Hollis/Milford has a *real* deadline. Most agents mark this moment with a generic "Back to School!" post. Forgettable. We can own it with a sharper frame.

**The idea:** Don't sell urgency — show arithmetic. Borrow a **scoreboard** from Chris's sports world: two numbers in tension, like a game clock.

> **Days until the first bell in Amherst: ~32**
> **Days left to go under contract and still make it: ~10**

That gap *is* the hook. The first number is the school calendar. The second is the closing calendar (30–45 day close + settle in before late-August start). The tension between them does the persuading — no fake scarcity, just honest math a parent feels in their stomach.

**Why it's strong:**
- **Real deadline, not manufactured.** It's a countdown that's actually true, which is the only kind that keeps our credibility.
- **Highest-intent segment, perfect moment.** Family buyers who want the school year to start right are the most motivated buyers we'll see all summer, and this is *their* week.
- **Distinctive treatment.** "Back to school" is wallpaper. A two-number scoreboard is a scroll-stopper and unmistakably ours (sports DNA is very Chris).
- **Emotionally true.** The pull isn't the first day — it's the 180 days after it: the bus stop, the new friends, walking in already belonging. Say that.

**How to run with it (executable today):**
1. **Chris — one short-form video.** To camera, or over B-roll of a school/empty bus. One honest message: "If you want your kid starting the year in [town], the clock that matters isn't the school calendar — it's the closing calendar, and it's running." Soft CTA: "If that's you, let's talk *this week*." 45 sec, shoot in one take.
2. **Fiona — the graphic.** The scoreboard as a static tile (cobalt/sunflower): two big numbers stacked, the gap highlighted in yellow. Works as a standalone post + the video end-card. Reusable weekly — the numbers just tick down.
3. **Jack (optional assist).** Same frame as an email subject line to any family-buyer segment: *"You have about 10 days for this to still work."*

**One guardrail:** I used ~Aug 26–28 as the SAU 39 start — **Chris should confirm the exact first-day date** before we post a specific number. Don't assert it if we're not sure; "late August" is safe until confirmed.
