## 2. Quick correctness catch on the Aug 6 blog draft (before it publishes)

Two things that'll read as sloppy if they go live as-is:
- **Date error:** "forecasts suggest they'll ease through the end of **2024**" — we're in 2026. Should be 2026/2027. Easy fix, but it torpedoes credibility on a *rates* post.
- **Image alt text:** "New England **coastal** great room… navy island kitchen." Coastal is our retired Florida voice — inland Hillsborough County isn't coastal. Swap "coastal" for "farmhouse" or "colonial."
- **Minor, worth a glance:** the blog cites Nashua DOM 30 / statewide 53 days, while your same-week newsletter cites NH DOM ~26 days. Not wrong necessarily (different cuts/sources), but two pieces going out the same week with different day counts invites a "which is it?" — worth reconciling or footnoting the source split.

The blog's core argument is strong; these are just three specks on a clean shirt.
