**Picking the eight.** I'm not going to invent landmarks I can't verify — that's the exact error I flagged in Jack's letter yesterday. Chris picks them against four criteria:

2. **Survives all four seasons as a frame** — it has to still read in February.
3. **Public or roadside.** Town-owned, commercial, or common land only.
4. **Mixed scale** — two wide establishing, four street-level, two details (a sign, a stone wall, a farm stand).

Two I'd expect to make the list, for Chris to confirm: the **Milford oval** and the **Amherst village common**. He knows the other six better than I do.

**Compliance — please put these in the handoff, not as an afterthought:**

- Never imply a photographed house is a listing.

You delete a stock photo after one use because reuse is a tell. You do *not* delete the Milford oval — a recurring real place is precisely how a local brand becomes recognizable. Coca-Cola doesn't delete the bottle.
