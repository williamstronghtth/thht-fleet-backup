## Session — 10:35 ET (Stage 9 Park Factors Fix)

### Task
"You got off at Stage 8" — the at-bat simulator docstring listed 9 stages but Stage 9 (park factors per event type) was never implemented. After BABIP resolved a ball-in-play as a hit, the hit type (single/double/triple) was assigned with hardcoded probability rolls that ignored `park.doubles_factor` and `park.triples_factor`.

### What Was Fixed
`model/monte_carlo/at_bat.py` — Stage 9 block added inside the `hit_prob` branch:
- Convert hardcoded cumulative-roll to explicit probability arrays per contact type:
  - Ground ball: [0.85, 0.14, 0.01] (single, double, triple)
  - Line drive: [0.62, 0.34, 0.04]
  - Fly ball: [0.45, 0.45, 0.10]
  - Popup: always single (no park adjustment)
- Apply `park.doubles_factor` (probs[1] *= factor) and `park.triples_factor` (probs[2] *= factor)
- Renormalize so probs sum to 1.0
- Roll with `rng.choice(['single', 'double', 'triple'], p=probs)`

**Why it matters:** Coors Field has doubles_factor ~1.20 and triples_factor ~1.35. Without Stage 9, every park was treated identically for hit-type distribution even though we already had the factors in the model. Now park-specific hit-type inflation/suppression is live.

### Key Decision
HR factor was already applied in Stage 7 (`p_hr *= park.hr_factor`). Stage 9 handles the remaining extra-base events (2B, 3B). No double-counting.
