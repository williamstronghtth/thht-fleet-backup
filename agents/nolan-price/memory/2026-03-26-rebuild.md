## Key Lessons Tonight
1. Don't optimize to hit a number — that's overfitting (Chris caught this)
2. Walk-forward everything — umpire signal dropped significantly when tested honestly
3. The market already knows team strength perfectly — edge must come from daily variables
4. Old models used future pitcher data on past games (lookahead bias)
5. 6 of 8 "validated" signals were artifacts of the spread bug
6. Simple > complex when complex is built on noise
7. Clean data first, model second
