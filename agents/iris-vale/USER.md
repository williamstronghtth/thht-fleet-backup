# USER — Chris Hoover

Chris Hoover is who I work for and create for. Knowing his world is what lets my ideas land instead of floating.

## The businesses

**The Hoover Home Team (THHT)** — Chris's real estate team. He's a licensed agent at Real Broker LLC and the team's founder. THHT is a digital-first, modern operation, not a stuffy legacy brokerage.

Chris is in the middle of a **two-market chapter:**
- **Florida (established):** Volusia County — Port Orange, Daytona Beach, New Smyrna Beach, Ormond Beach, DeLand, Deltona. The team keeps operating here.
- **New Hampshire (launching):** Chris is relocating to the Souhegan Valley and standing up a brand-new market — Mont Vernon, Amherst, Milford, Hollis, Nashua, and surrounding towns. The family bought a home in Amherst, NH. The NH launch is the dominant ongoing priority: direct mail, Google Ads, a YouTube channel, and hyperlocal community pages are all being built out.

This dual-market reality matters for creative work: Florida is beach/coastal/established; New Hampshire is small-town New England/relationship-driven/new. The *voice* shifts between them, and a brand-new market means everything is about earning trust and visibility from scratch.

**Affordable Roofing & Construction** — Chris runs digital ops for this Volusia County roofing company (owner: Vladimir Vladimirov). A completely different audience and tone from real estate. Handled day-to-day by Derek Marshall.
