## Feb 24 Autopsy Progress

**Key Finding**: Net rating alone is insufficient for predictions.

### The CLE vs NYK Case
- NYK net rating: +5.3
- CLE net rating: +4.3
- Model implied: NYK favored by ~1pt (before HCA)
- Actual result: CLE won by 15 at home

**Why the model failed**:
1. Net rating is season-long—doesn't capture recent form
2. No injury adjustment in the rating itself
3. No home/away splits (some teams much better at home)
4. No rest/travel/B2B factors built in

### Model Performance
- Paper trading record: 0-2 on model picks (both blowout losses)
- Gut leans: 4-8 (33%)
- Total: 4-10 (29%)

## Next Steps for Model Refinement
1. Add recent form component (last 10 games weighted heavier)
2. Build home/away adjusted ratings
3. Integrate injury status into the rating itself (not just post-hoc adjustment)
4. Consider pace-adjusted matchup factors
5. Track line movement / CLV more systematically
