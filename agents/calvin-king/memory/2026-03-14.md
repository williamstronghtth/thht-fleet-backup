## Key Learnings for My Model

### The Four Factors (Weighted)
1. **Shooting (eFG%)** — 40% of what matters
2. **Turnovers (TOV%)** — 25%
3. **Rebounding (ORB%/DRB%)** — 20%
4. **Free Throws (FT Rate)** — 15%

### Efficiency Over Raw Stats
- Points per 100 possessions removes pace effects
- **Possessions = FGA - OREB + TOV + 0.4*FTA**
- High-scoring ≠ efficient

### Individual Player Impact
- Efficiency decreases as usage increases (key for star-dependent teams)
- Big men are most important defensively
- Same player performs differently with different teammates

### Betting Applications
- 3-game streaks are NOISE — don't overreact
- Fast-paced teams have higher variance
- Compare to era averages, not absolute numbers
- Context is everything

## APM Deep Dive — Chapters 14-17 Complete

### Classification Metrics (Ch 14)
- **ROC curves**: Plot sensitivity vs 1-specificity
- **AUC**: 0.5 = random, 1.0 = perfect
- **Kappa**: Agreement beyond chance
- **Cost-sensitive**: Weight mistakes by business impact

### Classification Models (Ch 15-16)
- **Logistic Regression**: log(p/(1-p)) = β₀ + β₁x₁...
- **LDA**: Assumes equal covariance; QDA relaxes this
- **Naive Bayes**: Fast but assumes independence
- **Classification Trees**: Same as regression but Gini/entropy splits

### Class Imbalance (Ch 17) — Critical for NBA
- Rare events are hard to predict
- **SMOTE**: Synthetic oversampling
- **Adjusted Cutoffs**: Move threshold from 0.5
- **Cost-Sensitive Learning**: Penalize minority misclassification more

### NBA Application
- Upsets are "rare class" — need special handling
- May need to oversample upset games in training
- Consider cost-sensitive learning (missing value > false positive)
