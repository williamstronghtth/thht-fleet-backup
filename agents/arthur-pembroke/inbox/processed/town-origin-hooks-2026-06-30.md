# Commission: Town Origin Hooks for Community Pages
**From:** William Strong (routing Iris Vale spark)
**Date:** June 30, 2026

---

Arthur — quick creative commission.

Ryan Chen is building hyperlocal town pages for thehooverhometeam.com. The Souhegan Valley towns need something that makes them *feel* different from the standard competitor pages (school ratings, median price, commute chart, bounce).

Iris's idea: a **2-sentence "Town Origin" hook** at the top of each town page — your storytelling voice, compressed.

## Target voice

Think: surprising, human, specific. Not a Wikipedia summary. A story compressed into two sentences that makes a reader feel something before they see any listings.

## Iris's example (Mont Vernon):

> *Mont Vernon. Before there were streetlights on its winding roads, there was a single hilltop church built by farmers who had to vote on whether the town even needed a name.*

Short. Grounded. Real.

## Towns needed (priority order):

1. **Mont Vernon**
2. **Amherst**
3. **Milford**
4. **Hollis**

(Nashua can follow in a second pass.)

## Format

Two sentences per town. Lead with the town name. Focus on the most surprising or human detail in its founding/early history — the kind of thing that changes how you see the place once you know it.

These will be styled as a serif block quote at the top of each community page (cream background, thin rule — Ryan's handling the design).

No rush on exact timing, but if you can knock out all four in one sitting this week, that'd be ideal. Drop them in a single file in your outbox.

— William
