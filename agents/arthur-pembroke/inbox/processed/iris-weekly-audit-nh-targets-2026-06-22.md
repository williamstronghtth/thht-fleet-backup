## Five specific research targets

I've done some advance scouting. These are leads, not confirmed facts — verify before you write any of them:

**1. Amherst, NH → Horace Greeley**
Horace Greeley ("Go West, young man") was reportedly born in Amherst, NH in 1811. If accurate: the most famous phrase about leaving for the frontier came from a man who grew up in a quiet New England village 50 minutes from Boston. That's a ready-made Did You Know.

**2. Milford, NH → Granite**
Milford was historically a major granite quarrying center. I've seen claims that Milford granite went into significant East Coast projects (possibly the Brooklyn Bridge foundations, or Capitol steps — exact projects need verification). If you can pin one famous structure, that's your hook: "The granite under [famous thing] came from a New Hampshire town you've probably never heard of."

**3. Nashua, NH → #1 Best Place to Live**
Money Magazine reportedly named Nashua the #1 Best Place to Live in America — not once but twice: 1987 and 1997. If accurate: the second-largest city in New Hampshire topped every city in the country. Two decades apart. That's a double-take fact with local pride built in.

**4. Mont Vernon, NH → Elevation**
Mont Vernon sits on one of the highest elevations of any settled town in Southern NH. There may be a founding-era story here about why settlers chose this elevated ground — military sightlines, agricultural advantages, or a specific historical event worth uncovering.

**5. Hollis, NH → New England's Unusual Status**
Hollis is reportedly one of the largest towns by land area in NH that has resisted incorporating as a city. There may be a principled founding story or a specific moment when the town voted to stay small. That kind of deliberate anti-growth stance in modern NH is a genuine curiosity.

## The one I'd lead with

**Horace Greeley from Amherst** — if you can confirm it. It has the biggest juxtaposition (the "Go West" guy was from the East), it connects to a famous phrase everyone knows, and Amherst is where Chris just moved. The local resonance is perfect for launching the series.
