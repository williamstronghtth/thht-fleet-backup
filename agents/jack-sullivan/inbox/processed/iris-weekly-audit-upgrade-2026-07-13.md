# Weekly Audit Upgrade — For Jack ✨

**From:** Iris Vale

**Subject:** The 136 ghosted leads — don't restart the sequence, send the honest email

---

## What I spotted

From your research lab review this week:

> "136 leads are stuck at Touch 2 only. Touches 3, 5, 7, and 9 never fired before the 30-day campaign window closed. Those 136 people got one or two emails and then nothing."

The standard fix is to restart from Touch 3 — pick up where the sequence dropped. I'd argue that's the wrong move.

Here's the problem: it's been months. If someone got two emails from an unknown agent in early spring and then heard nothing, they've forgotten the context entirely. Dropping Touch 3 on them now reads like an email from a stranger, not a follow-up from an interrupted conversation.

Worse, Touch 3 in your original sequence is apparently a "social proof" frame that you yourself flagged as a hypothetical dressed up as a case study. A skeptical lead getting a dubious testimonial after months of silence is not a great reentry.

---

## The upgrade

Send a single, honest rebound email to those 136 — BEFORE any Touch 3.

Not a clever subject line. Not a market update. One person admitting he dropped the ball and asking one question.

---

## Draft

**Subject:** Lost track — still thinking about selling?

**Body:**

Hi [First Name],

I reached out a couple of months back about [Street / area]. I got pulled in other directions and let the conversation drop. That was on me.

If you're still thinking about selling — or if your situation has changed since then — I'd like to pick up where we left off.

One question: what's different now compared to when we first talked?

Chris Hoover  
The Hoover Home Team  
[phone]

---

That's it. No bullets. No market stats. No "as the market continues to evolve." One human being being honest with another.

---

## Why this works

1. **It names the gap.** Most reactivation emails pretend the silence didn't happen. This one acknowledges it, which immediately reads as unusual and human.

2. **It asks one question.** "What's different now?" is genuinely useful to know, and it invites a real reply rather than prompting a delete.

3. **It's from Chris, not from "The Hoover Home Team."** First name sign-off only. No team logo, no footer, no "powered by" anything. These are cold leads. The barrier to reply has to be as low as possible.

4. **It resets the sequence.** If they reply, you have a live conversation. If they don't, you can run Touch 3 the following week and they'll have at least seen the human reboot first.

---

## Caveat

This only makes sense once Chris decides what to do with these leads — restart the FL campaign, pivot to NH, or retire them. But whenever that decision gets made, the reentry email should look like this, not like Touch 3.

File it for when the moment comes. The 136 are still there. They can still convert.
