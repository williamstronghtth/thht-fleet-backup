### Market Context

Nashua is the most competitive sub-market in Hillsborough County — and possibly all of Southern NH. With 0.29 months of supply and homes averaging just 10–12 days on market, buyers need to be pre-approved, decisive, and flexible on terms. The single-family median has jumped from $538,500 to $600,000 year-over-year — an 11.4% gain — while condos (~$450K) represent the entry point for first-time buyers.

**What's driving prices:** No state income tax, proximity to Boston via Route 3/I-93, strong employment base (BAE Systems, Oracle, Southern NH University), and a limited housing supply relative to demand.

**New construction relief on the way:** Nashua has the largest new construction pipeline in the county — approximately 1,918 units permitted or in development. This will gradually soften the entry-level market over the next 18–24 months, but won't meaningfully change seller leverage this spring.

**Prospecting angle:** Sellers in Nashua are sitting on significant equity gains (11%+ in one year). Any homeowner who bought 3–7 years ago has seen major appreciation and may be ready to upsize, relocate, or cash out.

## 📋 Community & Local News

### 🎉 Marquee Event: Renaissance on the Riverwalk (May 22–25)
Nashua's **newly renovated Renaissance Park** (23 Water St) officially opens to the public this Memorial Day weekend with a 4-day festival:
- Live music, food trucks, vendors, and make-and-take art
- **Rubber Duck Regatta** on the Nashua River (Saturday)
- **Season-opening Farmers Market** (Sunday)
- **Drone show** over the river + lighted kayak parade (Saturday evening)
- **Mural dedication** celebrating Nashua's French-Canadian heritage (Saturday, May 23)
- Most activities free; food/drink for purchase

This is the biggest community activation Nashua has seen this spring. The park renovation represents the city's continued investment in its downtown riverfront identity — a direct lifestyle selling point for buyers considering urban NH living.

### 🏙️ Downtown Economy & Business Climate
- Nashua holds **10 distinct business districts**, from the downtown Millyard to retail corridors
- **Great American Downtown (GAD)** nonprofit actively drives downtown vitality programs and events
- City offers **Economic Revitalization Zone tax credits** for growing businesses (Business Profits Tax + Business Enterprise Tax)
- **Opportunity Zone** designation attracts investment capital to key neighborhoods
- NH's zero income tax + zero sales tax makes Nashua particularly compelling for business owners and employees relocating from MA

### 🎓 Community Life
- **Family STEM Day** was held May 16 at Nashua High School South — strong STEM community investment signal
- **Hampton Beach Bus** ($10 tickets) launched May 18 for summer season — coastal lifestyle accessible from Nashua
- **Teen Leadership Group** (ages 13–17) active and meeting May 27

1. **2018–2021 buyers** — Bought at $350–450K, now worth $550–600K+. Prime equity-harvest candidates. Ideal for move-up or relocation outreach.
