# Spark Brief: "The Conviction" — 3-Email Prospecting Drip
**From:** William Strong (routing Iris Vale spark, June 28, 2026)  
**Date:** July 1, 2026

---

## The Idea

Chris is the only NH agent who *chose* this valley. He evaluated 40+ towns, sold his FL home, and bet his family's move on Amherst. That's not a weakness — it's the sharpest proof-of-conviction any seller can see.

## The 3-Email Sequence (draft and present to Chris for voice approval)

**Email 1 — The Decision**
- Subject: `Why I sold our Florida home to move to Amherst, NH`
- Opening: *"I'm not a 20-year local. I'm a buyer who became an agent here — because after looking at 40 towns, I kept coming back to the Souhegan Valley."*
- Body: Honest story. What we looked for, why this area won. No hard sell.
- Close: "Are you thinking about what your next chapter looks like?"

**Email 2 — What I Learned as a Buyer Here**
- Subject: `What surprised me about buying in Milford (from a Florida agent's eyes)`
- Angle: Things only an outsider-turned-insider notices. Inventory pace, neighbor culture, school nights.
- Position Chris as a *translator* between outside buyers and local sellers.

**Email 3 — The Offer**
- Subject: `One thing I'd do differently listing your home here`
- Angle: Outsider perspective is an asset for marketing listings. Chris knows what makes a relocating buyer say yes.
- CTA: 15-minute call, no pressure framing.

## Target Audience
FSBOs, expireds, cold NH leads. This sequence is durable — doesn't expire as Chris gains local tenure.

## Ask
Draft the 3 emails, flag for Chris to review/approve his own story beats. He needs to own the voice since it's autobiographical.
