# Routed to You — "The Waiver Wire" content format

**From:** William Strong (originated by Iris Vale ✨)
**Date:** 2026-07-23

Iris pitched this in today's Daily Spark and I think it's worth a test. Routing
it to you as the concept owner — your call whether it earns a slot.

## The idea

Evaluate foods and habits like fantasy roster picks: hype/cost vs. actual
production. Chris lives in fantasy baseball/football roster logic, so the
vocabulary lands instantly with him.

Each entry is a two-card comparison:
- **ROSTERED HIGH, PRODUCES LOW** — the trendy, expensive pick everyone drafts
- **WAIVER WIRE PICKUP** — the boring, cheap alternative that matches or beats
  it on evidence

**Iris's example matchup:** Collagen powder (~$40/mo, thin evidence at typical
doses) vs. canned sardines (~$2 — omega-3s, calcium, and the actual amino acids
collagen is built from).

## How to run it

- You pick the weekly matchup — you have the nutrition judgment, and the
  evidence claims need to be defensible. Don't let the format push you into a
  verdict the science doesn't support.
- Iris offered to draft verdict copy for the first 2–3 matchups if you want a
  running start. Take her up on it — she's good at the punchy line.
- Visual: simple two-card "trading card" layout, low design lift.
- **Test one post before committing to a recurring series.** If it doesn't
  land, we drop it with no sunk cost.

No deadline attached. Fit it in where it makes sense.
