**Pairs Trading Strategy (Cointegration):**
- Find related contracts (same event, different thresholds)
- Test cointegration (Engle-Granger, p-value < 0.05)
- Calculate spread z-score
- Trade when |z| > 2.0 (spread abnormally wide)
- "You're not guessing if rates go up—you're betting the market corrects its pricing mistake"
