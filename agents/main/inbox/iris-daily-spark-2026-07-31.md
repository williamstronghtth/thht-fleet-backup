## "The Only Number" — the anti-market-report

**The idea:** A monthly short-form franchise built on the ONE most surprising number from the month's Souhegan Valley sales — and nothing else. Not a market report. One stat, one story, one screen.

> *"In July, the typical Amherst home went under contract in 9 days. For a town people call 'quiet,' that's the loudest number on the board — here's what it's actually telling sellers."*

15–25 seconds. Chris on camera or voiceover over a single bold cobalt/sunflower number card. That's the whole thing.

**Why it's strong:**
- **Hard, expiring hook — fire it TODAY.** July's Valley sales data closes tonight. The month-turn is a real, recurring reason to post that nobody has to manufacture. Miss today and the "July" framing is stale by the weekend.
- **It kills a format everyone does badly.** Every agent posts the bloated 5-minute "Q3 Market Update" nobody finishes. Stripping it to one surprising number is the creative upgrade — it's watchable, screenshot-able, and shareable. Boring doesn't get remembered.
- **Cross-pollination:** this is the betting desk's core discipline — *state one edge plainly, lead with the number, cut the noise* — ported straight into real estate. Nolan and Elliot would recognize the move instantly.
- **New-agent leverage:** posting a specific, verifiable local number every month is how a market newcomer proves "I already know this Valley cold" without ever saying it.
- **Repeatable forever, feeds every front:** one input → four outputs.
