## The Spark: The Rookie Card

**One-liner:** Script and shoot YouTube Video 1 this weekend — the only video that can only ever be made this week.

---

### The idea

Chris has been in Amherst for 3 days. The YouTube channel hasn't launched. Right now there is exactly one video that no other NH agent on earth can ever make, and it expires by July 10: *"I Just Moved to Amherst, NH — Here's My First Impression."*

Not a market report. Not an agent intro. The opening line of the channel is:

> *"I don't know every restaurant yet. I haven't been to every town. I moved here four days ago — and I'm going to tell you what I've seen."*

That's it. That's the whole premise. Film it this weekend while everything is still genuinely new. Thirty seconds of driving through downtown. A parking-lot observation about something that surprised him. One honest moment with the house, the street, the summer light. No script beyond the structure. The rawness is the product.

---

### Why this has to be Video 1

Every agent builds a YouTube channel by explaining their market. Chris can do that starting Video 2. But he can **never again be new here** — and that's his single biggest differentiator from every established NH agent.

A buyer searching *"moving to Amherst NH"* wants someone who just went through exactly what they're about to go through. That buyer is Chris's whole YouTube audience. And the only agent who can speak to them from authentic, recent, documented experience is the one who filmed it while it was still real.

**Sports parallel:** In trading cards, the rookie card is always the most valuable — because it captures the before. The moment before the player became a veteran, before the stats accumulated, before everyone knew the story. Chris has a one-time rookie card to film. It's worth more than any polished market report he'll ever make, because it can never be replicated.

---

### Window

- **This weekend:** July 5–6. Chris is still discovering things. Every street is still slightly unfamiliar. That texture shows on camera.
- **By July 10:** The newness starts to fade. He'll know where the grocery store is. The "I just moved here" framing starts to feel forced.
- **By August 1:** The window is closed. He's a local now. This video can't be made.

---

### Execution

- **William:** Write a light scene structure (not a script — just beats). Opening: Chris in the car, first line. Middle: 2–3 driving/walking observations about what he noticed in his first few days. Close: one honest line about why the family chose this place.
- **Chris:** Shoot on Canon 70D + iPhone (mix is fine here). One 45-minute session this weekend. No crew. No studio. The neighborhood is the set.
- **Fiona:** Title and thumbnail after footage comes in. Primary title format: *"I Just Moved to Amherst, NH — First Impressions (2026)"* — this is the search query, not the clever title.
- **Length:** 8–12 minutes YouTube. Also pull a 60-second Reel cut.

---

### One constraint to protect

Do not let anyone polish this into a standard agent video. No lower thirds. No music swell. No "hi I'm Chris Hoover with The Hoover Home Team." The magic is the first-person authenticity. The moment it starts to look like a real estate ad, it becomes just another real estate ad.
