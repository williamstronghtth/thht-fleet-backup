## The Spark: The Neighbor Letter

**One-line:** Chris sends handwritten Twriter cards to his 50 nearest Amherst neighbors this week — not as an agent, but as a new neighbor.

---

### The idea

Chris moves to Amherst today. Within the next 7 days — before he knocks on a single door with a business card, before any Google Ad runs, before the YouTube channel has one subscriber — he mails 50–75 handwritten cards to the homes nearest his new address. Pulled from county assessor data the same way Jack pulls absentee-owner lists.

The card says something like:

> *Hi — I'm Chris. My wife Audrey and I just moved to [neighborhood]. We couldn't be more excited to be here.*
> *If you ever need anything, we're around.*
> *— Chris Hoover | (phone)*

No logo. No "licensed realtor." No website. Just a name and a number on a Twriter card.

That's the whole move.

---

### Why it works

NH small-town real estate runs on relationships, not reach. Every other agent in Souhegan Valley prospects: cold calls, door knocks, mailers with headshots and "JUST LISTED" banners. Chris introduces himself as a *neighbor* — human first, agent never mentioned. Word travels: *"Did you meet the new guy on [street]? He's a realtor but he didn't make it weird — he just said hi."* That sentence is worth more than 500 ad impressions.

The "new neighbor" credibility window is the first 30 days. After that, he's just another agent who moved here. This idea expires fast.

---

### Cross-pollination source

Derek Marshall's philosophy for roofing after a storm: show up at the door and ask *"Are you okay?"* before you ever mention a roof. The job that follows is almost a formality because the human moment already happened. Same principle, different context — Chris's move is his "storm" entry point into this community.

### Execution (Jack + Chris)

- **Jack:** Pull the 50–75 nearest addresses to Chris's Amherst home from Hillsborough County assessor data. Export a clean mailing list. No filtering needed — this is neighbor density, not prospect scoring.
- **Chris:** Run the list through the Twriter. Write the card by hand (or Twriter-assist). Mail this week — before July 7.
- **Cost:** ~$35 in stamps. Zero printing cost. One afternoon.

---

### What it is NOT

This is not a prospecting mailer. Do not add a QR code, a listing, a "free home valuation," or a tagline. The second it looks like marketing, it loses everything. The power is in what it *doesn't* say.
