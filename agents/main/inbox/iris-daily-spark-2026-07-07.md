**The idea: Go sleeper-hunting, not star-hunting.**

Everyone following the Ken Pozek YouTube playbook targets the same towns. Buyers search "living in Amherst NH" and find four competing agent videos. That's the first-round pick everyone drafts — familiar, obvious, already crowded.

In fantasy sports, the draft gets won in rounds 4–7: the players projected to break out who everyone else ignored. You don't compete for the first-round pick. You find the value nobody else saw coming.

Applied to the NH YouTube channel: before filming the obvious Amherst/Nashua videos, spend 20 minutes searching YouTube for "living in Milford NH," "Mont Vernon New Hampshire," "Wilton NH real estate." I'd bet most of those searches return zero real estate results — or one thin video from 2019.

**The play:** Let the other agents fight over Amherst. Chris plants a flag in 3–4 of the Souhegan Valley's smaller towns with zero YouTube presence and becomes the only voice there. One video on "why people actually move to Milford, NH" ranks #1 by default — and because YouTube is also a Google signal, it surfaces in web search too.

**How to run with it:** Pull the YouTube search string for 5 Souhegan Valley towns (Milford, Wilton, Mont Vernon, Hollis, Mason). If any of them have under 3 real estate/lifestyle videos, that's the draft board. Film the lowest-competition town first, before the channel has any authority — those early videos earn wins fastest when the bar is low.

The Amherst video can come later, from a position of strength.
