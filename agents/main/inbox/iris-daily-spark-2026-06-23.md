## The Idea: "Florida Eyes"

Every video in Chris's YouTube opening chapter gets built around **one persistent frame: a Floridian discovering New Hampshire for the first time.**

Not as a gimmick. As a genuine narrative identity.

He's *actually* from Florida. He *actually* has never lived through a NH fall, never shoveled a granite-state driveway, never watched a town select board meeting, never seen what $450K buys in Amherst vs. what it buys in Port Orange. That's not a weakness to hide — it's the most compelling thing about him in this market. Outsider-turned-insider is a story arc people will follow.

**What this looks like in practice:**

Every video title starts from that vantage point — no manufactured curiosity required:
- *"A Floridian's First Fall in New Hampshire (I Wasn't Ready)"*
- *"What $500K Gets You in New Hampshire vs. Florida — Same Budget, Different World"*
- *"Why We Traded Year-Round Sun for Four Seasons — And Don't Regret It"*
- *"The Thing Nobody Told Me About Buying a Home in NH as an Outsider"*

**Why this works:**

1. **Two audiences, zero extra work.** Floridians dreaming about moving north will binge it. NH locals will watch because people love seeing their home through fresh eyes. Most NH real estate channels talk to one audience. This one earns two.

2. **The arc is already written.** Year one of content practically plans itself: arrival → first fall → first town meeting → first winter → first mud season → first town gathering. Chris doesn't have to invent topics — he just has to experience things he's never experienced before and put a camera on it.

3. **It's true.** Nobody can fake genuine discovery. This is the rare format where authenticity IS the content strategy. Ken Pozek plays the expert; Chris plays the fellow human figuring it out. Different positioning, completely uncontested.

4. **It answers the channel name question.** Suggested name: **"Florida to NH"** — or even more directional: **"Sunshine to Granite."** Either communicates the identity instantly, is searchable (people Google "moving from Florida to New Hampshire" more than you'd think), and gives every future thumbnail a built-in contrast visual: FL sunshine ↔ NH seasons.
