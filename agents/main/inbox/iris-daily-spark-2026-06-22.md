**Idea: "Where in the Valley?" — turning Chris's KoalaKlub obsession into a weekly engagement engine**

Here's the cross-pollination: Chris plays competitive geolocation games with his friends. He's good at reading visual geography clues and identifying places from drone angles and street-level details. That exact skill — which he exercises for fun every week — is the basis for a recurring social series that makes him look like THE local authority in a market where he's still brand new.

**The concept:** Once a week, Chris posts a photo or short drone clip of a specific, visually distinct spot somewhere in the Souhegan Valley towns — a covered bridge detail, a stone wall on a farm road, a distinctive mountain view from a town common, a historic building at an unusual angle. No caption context, no location tag. Just: *"Where in the Valley is this? First one to name it wins bragging rights."*

Comments go wild. Locals who've lived there for 40 years compete to be first. The next day: reveal post with a brief, genuine note about that place — why it caught his eye, a quick fact about it, maybe a drone pull-back shot for the full picture.

**Why this wins:**

It solves the hardest problem a new agent faces — earning local credibility before you've done local deals. Instead of telling people he knows the area, Chris *demonstrates* it, one recognizable landmark at a time. Locals who play the game are now following him for next week's puzzle. People tag their neighbors to see if *they* can get it. Every reveal becomes a tiny love letter to a specific place — exactly the relational register that NH small-town culture responds to.

The production lift is zero. He's already shooting drone footage for other content. One clip → Instagram Stories poll (drop a guess) → Reels reveal with a 15-second voiceover. Fiona builds the template once and it runs itself.

**The deeper move:** In the geolocation game community, the whole joy is *I knew it before you did.* That's the same psychological engine as finding a neighborhood before it gets expensive. "Where in the Valley?" quietly recruits an audience that's already primed to think like early buyers.

**Route to:** Fiona (Stories/Reels template build), Chris directly (one clip to launch with — shoot this week). Could also become a YouTube Shorts version with zero extra work. First puzzle as early as this Thursday.
