## The Spark: "Guess the Town" — a geo-game Reel series for the Souhegan Valley

**One-liner:** Turn every NH town into a guessing-game Reel — a weekly scroll-stopper that makes Chris's account the de facto Souhegan Valley authority without ever sounding like a sales pitch.

---

### The idea

Chris plays KoalaKlub GeoSports — the competitive geography game where you identify locations from visual clues. He's good at it because he thinks in landmarks, sight lines, and town signatures.

That same instinct is a Reel format nobody in NH real estate is using.

The series: **"Which Souhegan Valley Town Is This?"**

Each Reel is 20–30 seconds:
- 0:00–0:18: B-roll of one town. No labels. Just the visual clues — the town green, the brick storefronts, the covered bridge if there is one, the church steeple at dusk. One question overlay at the start: *"Can you guess which NH town this is?"*
- 0:18–0:28: Reveal. Town name + one sharp fact. Not "Amherst has great schools." Something specific: *"Amherst, NH — incorporated 1760, still doesn't have a single traffic light."* The kind of thing a local would say.
- Caption: *"Drop your guess below 👇 — locals don't count 😄 #SouheganValley #NewHampshire #MoveToNH"*

That's the whole format. One town per week. Eight towns in the valley = two months of content with zero creative fatigue.

---

### Why this works

**The algorithm likes it.** Questions in captions and overlays drive comments. Comments drive reach. Geo-puzzles ("is that Milford or Hollis?") are compulsively answerable — people who know the area respond to feel smart; people who don't respond because they're curious. Both groups share with friends.

**It builds the right kind of authority.** Not "I'm a real estate expert." The implicit claim is: *this agent knows these towns well enough to make a game out of them.* That's harder to fake and more valuable to a buyer researching a move.

**Cross-pollination with the community pages.** Ryan is building thht-communities as hyperlocal town pages. Every "Guess the Town" Reel links in the bio to the corresponding community page — Amherst, Milford, Hollis, etc. The Reel drives Instagram traffic to the web property. The web property gives leads a place to go deeper. The two flywheels feed each other.

**It uses footage Chris already has.** He has 4th of July Amherst footage from yesterday. That's episode 1 — shot, free, already existing. The reveal line writes itself: *"Amherst, NH — where they still do the 4th of July the way it was meant to be done."* Fiona just needs to cut it.
