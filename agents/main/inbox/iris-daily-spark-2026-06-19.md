**Idea: "Did You Know?" — Arthur's historical storytelling method, transplanted to NH Reels**

Here's the cross-pollination: Arthur already does the hard work every morning — one surprising historical fact, one vivid story, made compelling. That *exact format* is sitting unused as a Reels machine for the NH launch.

**The concept:** A recurring short-form series where Chris drops a single hyperlocal NH historical fact in 30 seconds. Not a market update. Not a neighborhood tour. Just: *"I just moved to Amherst and I didn't know this..."* — and then one genuinely surprising thing about the town's past. Milford granite quarried for the Brooklyn Bridge. Mont Vernon's Civil War record. A founding-era tavern that still stands. The stuff that makes a local stop scrolling and tag three neighbors.

Arthur researches one NH historical gem per week — a tiny add-on to his existing workflow, since he's already hunting "on this day" facts. Fiona packages it into a Reel with a consistent visual style (same text overlay, same 4-second hook format she already uses). Chris records a one-sentence on-camera opener. That's the whole machine.

**Why this wins in NH specifically:**
Nobody is doing hyperlocal historical content for Souhegan Valley towns on social media. The bar is zero. A single Reel about Amherst's 1774 founding — told in 30 vivid seconds — will get shared to every local Facebook group and town subreddit before noon. History-as-content is the quietest trust-builder there is: it says *I respect where you're from* before Chris has sold a single NH house.

**The creative frame that makes it click:** Chris isn't the expert yet. He's the newcomer who's *obsessed* with learning. "I didn't know this until I moved here" is more honest, more relatable, and frankly more interesting than "Let me show you my market." It's the genuine angle — and it scales across every town in the Souhegan Valley indefinitely.

**How to run with it:** Route to Arthur (one NH historical gem per week, Slack-style note to Fiona) and Fiona (consistent Reel template — text overlay, 30 sec, Chris on camera for the hook). No additional production needed beyond what both agents already do.
