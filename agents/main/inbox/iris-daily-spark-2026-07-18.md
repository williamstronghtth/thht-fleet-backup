## "Guess the Town" — A Reel Series That Proves Local Knowledge Without Claiming It

**The concept:**
Chris shoots a 15-second Reel of one hyper-specific, visually distinctive detail from a Souhegan Valley town — the Amherst town green, a classic NH stone wall in Hollis, the Milford town hall granite steps, the apple orchard on the road into Mont Vernon — no text, no voiceover, just the image. Single overlay: **"Which Souhegan Valley town is this? 🏡"** Chris reveals the answer in the first pinned comment.

**Where this comes from:**
Chris plays KoalaKlub GeoSports — a competitive geolocation game where you look at a photo and guess where in the world it is. That exact mechanic, transplanted into NH real estate, becomes native-feeling content instead of a campaign. The game already wired into his brain shows up as a content series. That's the seam.

**Why it hits:**
Every new agent in a new market says *"I know this area."* This series *shows it*, one hyperlocal image at a time — without a single word of self-promotion. It also triggers the algorithm trifecta: comments (everyone wants to guess), shares (locals tagging friends who know), and saves (people building their NH town mental map). And it turns into a soft education tool for relocating buyers who don't know the difference between Milford and Mont Vernon yet.

**Execution (simple):**
- Chris shoots on the DJI Mini 4 Pro or Canon 70D — no editing needed beyond a trim and overlay
- One town per week; 6–8 episodes covers the core Souhegan Valley market
- Fiona handles the caption cadence: "Answer in the comments 👇" + one line about what makes this town feel distinct
- Start with Amherst (home base, most confident) — gives Chris a genuine "I live here" energy that's impossible to fake

**First episode:** The Amherst town green. Classic white church, stone wall, summer green. Even people outside NH recognize *that* as New England — and locals will immediately know exactly which green it is.
