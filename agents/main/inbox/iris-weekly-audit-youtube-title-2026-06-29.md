William,

This is a fast fix for a real problem. Script 004 (Boston commuters, six towns, publishes July 9) has excellent content inside it. The title options don't match the quality of what's in the script. Here's why and what to use instead.

## What I noticed

The three current title options:

1. "Best Towns in NH for Boston Commuters (Under 60 Min)"
2. "I Ranked 6 NH Towns for Boston Commuters — Here's the Winner"
3. "Live in NH, Work in Boston: The 6 Towns That Actually Make Sense"

These are keyword-correct. They're also entirely predictable. A Boston-area professional scrolling YouTube has already seen ten videos with this exact title structure. They won't click because the title tells them exactly what they'll get — and they've already gotten it from someone else.

The script has genuinely better material than the title reflects. The line buried in Point 6: *"It's the 'I'm coming from Wellesley' town on this list."* That line is punchy, funny, and specific in a way that none of the current titles are. The thumbnail could be built around it. The title should borrow that energy.

## Replacement options

**Option A (underrated-angle hook):**
`I Ranked NH's Best Commuter Towns. One of Them Nobody's Talking About.`

Why: "Nobody's talking about" is the signal for the exact audience you want — people who've already done surface-level research and want the edge. Works with a thumbnail showing a question-mark overlay on Londonderry. High CTR potential.

**Option B (specific-and-honest hook):**
`6 NH Towns for Boston Commuters — The Honest Ranking (One Will Surprise You)`

Why: "Honest" is doing heavy lifting here. Real estate content is infamous for hype. "Honest" framing positions Chris differently. "One Will Surprise You" creates mild suspense without being clickbait.

**Option C (newcomer-credential hook):**
`I Moved to NH From Florida. Here's Where I'd Tell a Boston Commuter to Look.`

Why: This uses Chris's story as the credential — the same "I made this bet" logic that powers the whole NH content strategy. It's the least keyword-dense of the three but the most differentiated. Attracts people who want a real person's perspective, not a ranked list.

## Thumbnail direction

The "Edit-Friendly Extras" section of the script already flags the Londonderry line as pull-quote #2: *"Londonderry is the most underrated town on this list."* That line should be on the thumbnail, not just in the video. Text overlay on a residential street shot. If the thumbnail tells you which town is underrated before you click, the video's job is to tell you why — that's a strong hook loop.

The script's suggested "cold-open clip" (Chris saying: *"Six towns. All under 60 minutes. And I'll tell you exactly which one fits your situation."*) is actually a strong thumbnail quote too. Cleaner than anything in the current title options.

## Recommendation

Go with Option A as the primary title, pair it with the Londonderry underrated line on the thumbnail. The combination of "nobody's talking about" (curiosity trigger in title) + which town (revealed only inside the video) is a complete hook loop.
