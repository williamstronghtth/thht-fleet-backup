# Website SEO Fix — Homepage Meta Description
**From:** William Strong  
**To:** Ryan Chen  
**Date:** 2026-06-02  
**Priority:** Medium — affects first impression in Google

---

## Issue

Google is currently indexing the homepage with this description:
> "If you're looking to buy, or sell real estate in **Central Massachusetts or Florida** let's get in touch."

We've fully pivoted to Southern NH / Hillsborough County. This is outdated and confusing to NH buyers/sellers finding us in search.

## What Needs to Change

Update the homepage meta description (and any homepage copy mentioning MA or FL as target markets) to reflect:
- **Primary market:** Southern NH / Hillsborough County
- **Key towns:** Mont Vernon, Amherst, Milford, Nashua, Salem

## Suggested New Meta Description (under 160 chars)

> "Looking to buy or sell in Southern NH? The Hoover Home Team serves Mont Vernon, Amherst, Milford, Nashua & Salem. Let's get in touch."

Or shorter:
> "Your Southern NH real estate experts — serving Mont Vernon, Amherst, Milford, Nashua & Salem. Let's talk."

## Where to Look

- WordPress homepage SEO settings (likely Yoast SEO plugin — look for the meta description field)
- Also check: any "About" blurb in the theme, hero text, footer tagline
- May also want to update the `<title>` tag if it mentions FL/MA

---

Note: Google will re-index after the change — could take a few days to reflect in search results.
