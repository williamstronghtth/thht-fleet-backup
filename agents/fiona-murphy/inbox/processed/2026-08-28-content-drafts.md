## 📄 FULL BLOG POST TEXT

**Title:** Southern NH Home Prices Hit All-Time High — What It Means for Your Market

**Body:**

Southern NH home prices just hit a milestone. In July 2026, the median single-family home price across New Hampshire reached $580,000 — an all-time record and a 5.5% jump year over year. For Hillsborough County specifically, the median sits at $548,392, up approximately 3% from last year. While national housing markets are showing signs of fatigue, Southern New Hampshire continues to defy the slowdown, and home prices tell that story.

## Why This Matters for Buyers

The silver lining in higher prices? Inventory is finally expanding. Hillsborough County has 1,494 active listings, an 8% increase from last year. Statewide, New Hampshire added 2,992 active homes — the highest count in roughly seven years. That's real relief for buyers who felt locked out of the market just months ago.

Twenty-four days on market remains the norm in Hillsborough County, meaning homes are still moving, but the pace is steadier than spring's frenzy. Fifty-eight percent of homes sell above asking price, up from 55%, showing that while there's more inventory, buyer demand has not cooled.

For buyers, this moment offers balance. You are no longer competing in the spring feeding frenzy, but homes are still attracting strong interest. Fall and winter typically bring fewer competing offers — your leverage window is opening.

## What Sellers Should Consider

If you are thinking about selling your Southern NH home, the timing calculus has shifted. All-time record prices mean strong equity for move-up buyers. However, the expanding inventory and slightly slower pace (24 days vs. previous years' rushing sales) mean pricing and positioning matter more than they did six months ago.

Nashua presents a particular signal: the median there dropped to $576,500, down 2.7% year over year and the lowest point since March. If you own in Nashua, a realistic price and sharp marketing will be essential to stand out.

Higher-end properties in Mont Vernon are experiencing longer marketing times — 47 days compared to the county average of 24. If you are selling a premium home, plan for a longer timeline and invest in professional staging and photography.

## The Broader Story

According to the New Hampshire Association of Realtors, the state's housing market remains in strong seller territory, even with improving inventory. The phrase "far from a balanced market" came from NHAR's own leadership as recently as mid-summer, and July data confirms it: 1.71 months of supply in Hillsborough County still sits well below the 4 to 6 month range that defines balance.

Meanwhile, mortgage rates are holding steady. The 30-year fixed mortgage averaged 6.66% for the week ending August 27 — essentially unchanged from the prior week and up only marginally over the past year. Forecasters at Fannie Mae project rates could dip below 6% by the fourth quarter, which would ease affordability pressure that has been squeezing buyers since 2022.

## Ready to Take Your Next Step?

Whether you are buying or selling in Southern New Hampshire, a market that is simultaneously hitting all-time price records and finally expanding inventory presents distinct opportunities. Higher prices reward sellers and move-up buyers with strong equity. Expanding inventory and slightly slower pace benefit buyers shopping for their primary home.

The moment to act is now. If you are ready to explore what your Southern NH home is worth or what the market holds for your next move, let's talk.
