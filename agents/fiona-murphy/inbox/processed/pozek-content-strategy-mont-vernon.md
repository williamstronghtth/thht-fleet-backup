# Ken Pozek Content Strategy — Mont Vernon Adaptation

## The Pozek Playbook (Orlando, 2016-2026)

Ken Pozek moved to Orlando with zero clients and zero network. He made 100 videos in 100 days — 80 videos shipped before the first lead came in. Result: The Orlando Real now has 7M+ views and generates leads continuously.

**The strategic shift:** He's not "real estate agent who does YouTube." He's a **media/lifestyle brand** that happens to have a real estate team attached. Content first. Real estate second. The audience builds before the sales funnel.

## His Six Content Pillars

1. **Neighborhood deep-dives** — "Is X a good place to live?" format: price breakdown, schools, lifestyle, commute. Every neighborhood gets its own treatment.
2. **Comparison videos** — "Luxury vs Affordable Neighborhoods," direct price/lifestyle comparisons. High search intent.
3. **Relocation content** — Cost of living, taxes, insurance, schools. The standard "moving here" FAQ.
4. **Market updates** — Monthly/quarterly data videos. Real numbers (median price, DOM, inventory).
5. **Lifestyle/non-real-estate** — Restaurants, theme parks, local culture. **This is the secret sauce** — builds audience before they need a house.
6. **Development/what's coming** — New construction, what's changing. Evergreen search + local interest.

## Your Advantage (vs. Pozek in 2016)

## Recommended Content Roadmap for Mont Vernon

### Neighborhood Deep-Dives (Highest Leverage)
- **Anchor:** "Is Mont Vernon a good place to live?" (2-3 min, full ecosystem — schools, lifestyle, pricing, proximity)
- **Town series:** Amherst, Milford, Bedford, Merrimack, Hollis, Brookline, Wilton, New Boston (individual "Is X good?" videos)
- **Comparison:** "Mont Vernon vs Amherst: which should you move to?" (schools, price, commute, vibe)
- **Relocation angle:** "Where Boston commuters actually buy in southern NH"
- **Market access:** "Best NH towns under 45 min to Manchester airport"

### Relocation Content (Your Unique Angle)
- **Personal narrative:** "Florida to New Hampshire: what nobody tells you before moving" ← This is YOU. Most NH agents can't make this.
- **Tax comparison:** "FL property taxes vs NH property taxes: the real math" (NH no income tax, high property tax)
- **Insurance deep-dive:** "Homeowners insurance: Florida vs New Hampshire" (seasonal risk differences)
- **Why video:** "Why Floridians are moving to New Hampshire in 2026" (data + your story)

### Lifestyle/Non-Real-Estate (Audience Building)
- Mont Vernon town meeting explained for newcomers
- Best apple orchards / fall foliage drives in Souhegan Valley
- "What a NH winter actually costs" (heating oil, plowing, tires, unexpected expenses)
- Best ski areas within 90 min of Mont Vernon, ranked
- The "603 lifestyle" — what it means to locals (short on culture/vibe)
- Restaurant tours: Milford, Amherst, Nashua
- Local events calendar (Memorial Day through fall festivals)

### Market Updates
- Monthly Hillsborough County market snapshot (median price, DOM, inventory)
- Quarterly "what's changed" videos
- New development projects (Nashua commercial, residential pipeline)

### Development/What's Coming
- New residential projects in Nashua, Milford, Amherst
- Infrastructure changes (Route 101 work, etc.)
- School expansions / town improvements

## Execution Notes

**Start with:** Neighborhood deep-dives + relocation content (these have built-in search demand and match your unique positioning)

**Build with:** Lifestyle content (lower production burden, builds audience long-term)

**Sustain with:** Monthly market updates (shows you're an expert, not salesy)

**Evergreen:** All of these are searchable, reusable, and rankable. Repurpose across YT, TikTok, Instagram, blog.
