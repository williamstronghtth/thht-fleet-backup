## MARKET SNAPSHOT

### Price & Inventory (Latest)
- **Median Sale Price:** $660,000 (↑ 39.8% YoY)
- **Price per Sq Ft:** $353 (↑ 13.3% YoY)
- **Average Home Value:** $594,844 (↑ 5.7% YoY)
- **Days on Market:** 9 days (↓ 47% from May 2025) — **VERY FAST SELLING**
- **Market Condition:** Very competitive, tight inventory

### Key Insight
Amherst homes are selling **47% faster than last year** with prices up nearly 40%. This is a **seller's advantage market** with strong momentum. Limited inventory + high buyer demand = rapid sales.
