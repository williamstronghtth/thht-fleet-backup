## Content

Something has changed in the Southern New Hampshire real estate market. Homes that once sold in 7-14 days are now taking 44 days on average to close. That might sound alarming, but here's what you need to know: this is a feature, not a bug.

Understanding the 44-Day Market

Hillsborough County is experiencing a significant market slowdown compared to the frenzy of 2022-2024. With inventory finally growing and buyer demand stabilizing, homes aren't vanishing in bidding wars anymore. The median home price is down 1.7% year-over-year ($540,917 in Q1 2026), and rates are holding steady at 6.25% APR.

For sellers, this means the days of "list and wait for multiple offers" are over. For buyers, this means real leverage is back. But both groups face a new reality: patience and strategy matter more than speed.

The Strategic Advantage

Well-positioned homes still move in 7-14 days. The difference between fast sales and the 44-day average comes down to three factors:

1. Pricing to Market: Homes priced 3-5% above market value linger for 60+ days. Homes priced right on day one attract serious buyers and close faster. In a balanced market, honest pricing wins.

2. Professional Photography and Staging: The homes selling fastest don't necessarily have the most expensive features. They have the best presentation. Virtual tours, professional photography, and strategic staging matter when buyers have choices.

3. Seller Urgency and Flexibility: Seller concessions are returning. Inspections are no longer waived. Appraisals that come in low are now real issues. Sellers who understand these shifts and adapt win faster sales.

What This Means for Your Move

If you're a seller in Mont Vernon, Amherst, Nashua, or Milford, the 44-day average isn't your destiny. It's the baseline for unprepared homes. Focus on pricing, presentation, and flexibility.

If you're a buyer, you finally have time to think. The days of waiving inspections and appraisals are gone. You have negotiating power you haven't had in two years. Use it strategically.

The market isn't crashing. It's stabilizing. Single-digit growth is the new normal, and that's sustainable. Whether the 44-day timeline works for you depends on how prepared you are.
