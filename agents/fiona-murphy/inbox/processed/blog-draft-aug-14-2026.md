## BODY CONTENT

Southern NH real estate moves fast. Really fast.

While the national average home spends 45 days on the market, homes in Hillsborough County are selling in just 23 to 24 days. That's 50% faster, and it's not a fluke. It's the result of three powerful market forces colliding at once.

### The Numbers Tell a Story

When buyers and sellers look at the Southern NH market right now, they see a market where speed equals strength. Homes in the southern NH home market fast sales are competing intensely. Over 50% sell above asking price. Inventory is tight at just 1.71 months of supply. This combination creates urgency for both sides of a transaction.

Milford leads the region as the speed champion, with homes selling in just 19 to 20 days. Mont Vernon follows at 27 days, still well below the national average.

### What's Driving the Speed?

**Constrained Inventory.** Despite an 8% year-over-year increase, Southern NH still has far fewer homes for sale than demand can support. [According to recent real estate market data](https://www.realtor.com/research/data/), tight inventory creates multiple-offer scenarios and faster sales cycles. Buyers have to move decisively when a property hits the market. Properties that are clean, inspected, and priced at market value sell faster.

**Strong Buyer Demand.** People are relocating to Southern NH. We see it every week. Buyers from Boston, New York, and across New England recognize the value of these communities. They're pre-approved and ready to make moves quickly. That demand creates the speed we're seeing.

**Market Momentum.** The Southern NH market is outperforming regional and national benchmarks. That momentum creates confidence. Sellers list, buyers compete, properties sell. Speed follows naturally.

### What This Means for Sellers

If you're selling, speed is your advantage. Homes that are clean, inspected, and listed at market value sell in the timeframe we're seeing. That speed means you're not competing for attention over weeks or months. You're competing for qualified buyers in days.

Plus, over 50% of homes in Hillsborough County are selling above asking price. That's because competition is real, inventory is scarce, and buyers are ready.

### What This Means for Buyers

If you're buying, speed matters differently. You need to be ready. Pre-approval, clear preferences, and quick decision making are table stakes in this market. The best homes don't stay listed long. Being prepared means you won't miss opportunity.

### The Bottom Line

Understanding the Southern NH market means understanding that timing and readiness determine outcomes. Whether you're buying or selling, the market rewards preparation.
