## 📊 MARKET SNAPSHOT

### Real Estate Market
- **Median Home Value:** $594,844 (↑ 5.7% YoY)
- **June 2026 Listing Median:** $749K
- **Days on Market:** ~32 days average
- **Market Status:** Balanced to slightly favorable for buyers in $500K-$900K range
- **Inventory Level:** Increased ~11% in early 2026; more options for buyers than past two years

**Market Takeaway:** After years of intense seller pressure, Amherst is now showing more balanced conditions. Well-priced homes in top school districts still move quickly, but buyers have more negotiating power and room to get inspections without losing offers.
