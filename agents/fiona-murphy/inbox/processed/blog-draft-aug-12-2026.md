## FULL POST CONTENT

### Opening Paragraph (includes keyphrase)

If you've been watching mortgage rates like a hawk waiting for a break, yesterday happened. Rates dropped 15 basis points in a single day, from 6.87% down to 6.61%. That might sound like a rounding error, but in real money terms on a Southern NH home purchase, it means something tangible: roughly $30,000 to $40,000 of additional buying power on a $500,000 home.

But here's what matters even more than the number itself: the *reason* for the drop signals a shift in market psychology. The Federal Reserve's recent communication suggests stability ahead, with rates expected to hold in the 6.3% to 6.5% range through the end of the year. After months of buyers paralyzed by "maybe rates will fall further," that stability is permission to move. August 2026 is the moment. Southern NH real estate market conditions for buyers have genuinely changed in the last 48 hours.

### H2: What This Rate Drop Means for Your Buying Power

Numbers alone don't tell the whole story. Let's translate 15 basis points into your real-world advantage.

On a $500,000 purchase price with a 20% down payment ($100,000) and a 30-year mortgage, the rate drop from 6.87% to 6.61% reduces your monthly payment by roughly $100. Over the life of the loan, that's over $36,000 in interest savings.

But here's the inverse calculation that matters more right now: with the same monthly payment budget you had at 6.87%, you can now afford a home that's $30,000 to $40,000 more expensive. In Hillsborough County's market, that tier shift opens up entire neighborhoods and price points that were out of reach just yesterday.

The catch? This advantage only holds if rates stay in this band. Fed signals suggest they will, but the market is watching closely. Buyers who act in August negotiate from strength. Buyers who wait for further drops risk another sideways market while their opportunity window closes.

### H2: Hillsborough County Real Estate Market Snapshot, August 2026

Here's what the Southern NH market looks like right now, in real data:

**Median Sale Price:** $484,000 — stable month-over-month

**Inventory:** Up 11% year-over-year, with 1.6 months of supply. That's the first structural shift toward buyer leverage we've seen all year. Sellers know inventory is climbing.

**Offers Per Property:** 2.1 — down from 2.3 just two weeks ago. Translation: homes are no longer drawing bidding wars. Offers are becoming negotiable again.

**Above-Ask Premium:** 54% of homes selling above asking price, down from 58% in early August. For the first time in 2026, *negotiation room* is appearing in the market.

**Days on Market:** 24 days, steady. Homes are still moving, but not at fire-sale speed. That's exactly the kind of balanced market where savvy buyers win.

The data tells a coherent story: the seller's momentum is cooling. Buyer leverage is rising. August through December is the off-season, when less attention means less competition and more flexibility from sellers. This is not desperation pricing. It's opportunity pricing.

### H2: Why August Through December Is the Buyer's Advantage Window

Real estate markets have seasons, and August through December is the one where buyers gain ground.

Spring and early summer (March through July) bring a flood of both buyers and sellers into the market. Competition is fierce. Sellers hold firm. Bidding wars are routine. If you're a buyer with any flexibility, that's not when you want to shop.

Fall and winter (August through December) tell a different story. Many sellers who didn't sell over the summer become motivated—their personal timelines haven't changed, but market conditions have. Many buyers step back into hibernation, assuming nothing moves in the cold months. That's wrong in a market like Southern NH, where our inventory is climbing and our community remains attractive year-round.

The psychologically perfect buyer is someone who moves in September or October: the market is still warm, but most competition has retreated. You negotiate *positions*, not just prices. Inspections close faster. Appraisals come in without drama. Sellers are ready to move.

If you've been waiting for August, you're exactly on schedule. The advantage is real. It's time.
