# Weekly Audit Upgrade — For Fiona ✨

**From:** Iris Vale  
**Date:** July 13, 2026  
**Subject:** The social feed needs one "field observation" slot per week — here's what it looks like

---

## What I spotted

This was your best operational week ever (7-for-7, clean narrative arc, real variety in topics). The process is locked. What needs attention is a creative pattern.

Every social post this week runs the same structural play:

**[stat hook] → [what this means for buyers/sellers] → [bullet points] → [CTA]**

"70% faster than the state average."  
"18 Days to Sold."  
"Summer is hot. And not just the weather."  
"Bidding wars are over. Negotiation is back."

These are accurate and useful. They're also starting to read like a market data feed, not a person who lives here. The audience can feel the difference even if they can't name it. And when something like "Summer is hot. And not just the weather." slips in — that sentence could appear in any real estate agent's July content anywhere in America — that's the tell.

Now read this, from William's July 7 newsletter:

> "A neighbor knocked on the door with blueberries from her garden on day two. Another stopped by to point out the trailhead for Purgatory Falls — literally five minutes from the house. The Fourth of July in a town that has 46 documented Revolutionary War heroes felt less like a holiday and more like a homecoming I didn't know I was attending."

That's Chris's voice from the field. It's the best paragraph the team has produced this month. It ran in a newsletter to 85 people. The social feed — which reaches hundreds, maybe thousands — got zero of it.

---

## The upgrade

Build **one "field observation" slot** into the weekly content calendar. One post per week that comes from a real thing Chris noticed or experienced while living in the Souhegan Valley — not from a market stat.

Not instead of the data posts. In addition to. One out of ten.

It becomes the post that earns the other nine.

---

## What it looks like

Here's a draft you can use or pitch Chris to riff on:

---

*"The trailhead for Purgatory Falls is five minutes from my house. I didn't know that the day I moved in. A neighbor knocked and told me.*

*That's the Souhegan Valley. You don't find it on Zillow. You find it when someone who's been here twenty years decides you're worth knowing.*

*If you're considering a move to Southern NH: what you're buying isn't a square footage number. It's that knock on the door.*

*(We can tell you what the square footages cost too. Link in bio.)"*

---

Or if Chris has a fresh observation from this week — something specific, something true, something you couldn't say from a desk in Boston — that's the post. One real moment. Maybe 100 words. No bullets.

---

## The ask

When you're building next week's calendar (July 14 queue is already full — no change needed), flag one slot in the week of July 20 as "Chris field observation" instead of another data angle. 

Ask him: *"What's one thing you've noticed in the last week that a person who doesn't live here wouldn't know?"*

Whatever he says, that's the post.

---

The data posts tell people Chris knows the market. The field observation posts tell people Chris knows the place. You need both. Right now you have nine of one and zero of the other.
