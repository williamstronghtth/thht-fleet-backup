## 🏘️ REAL ESTATE MARKET SNAPSHOT

### Bedford, NH vs. Hillsborough County — June/July 2026

| Metric | Bedford NH | Hillsborough County |
|---|---|---|
| **Median Sale Price** | ~$882,000–$900,000 | $548,453 |
| **YoY Price Change** | +2% | +5% |
| **Median Days on Market** | 12–17 days | 23 days |
| **Sale-to-List Ratio** | ~99–100% | ~62% close above asking |
| **Months of Supply** | ~1.5–2 months | 1.6 months |
| **Active Listings (County)** | — | ~1,482 (up 11% YoY, highest since 2021) |
| **30-yr Mortgage Rate (NH)** | ~6.375–6.56% | (down ~0.40% YoY) |

### Key Takeaways for Content:
- Bedford remains **one of the most competitive markets in Southern NH** — homes moving in under 2 weeks on average
- The sale-to-list ratio near 100% means sellers get asking price. Multiple-offer situations still common under $1.1M
- Premium pricing reflects top-5% school district (SAU #25), no-state-income-tax advantage, and I-293/US-101 commuter access
- Hillsborough County inventory up 11% YoY but still only 1.6 months of supply — far below the 4–6 months that signals balance
- County has appreciated **~99% since early 2019** — existing homeowners are sitting on significant equity
- Inventory improving slightly vs. 2025 — but prepared buyers still need to act fast
- New construction vs. resale is a **hot buyer question** in 2026
