## Market Context

Milford's real estate market remains **hot and competitive** — homes sell in under 2 weeks on average. The $99K gap between list and sale prices indicates strong negotiation leverage for well-priced properties.

**Prospecting angles:**
- **For sellers:** Capitalize on 12-13 day average timeline; market demand is strong
- **For investors:** Fast absorption rate signals stable rental demand
- **For relocators:** Boston-area professionals relocating to NH; strong commuter access via Route 101 to I-93

## Community & Business Profile

**Known as:** "The Granite Town" — historic granite industry heritage

**Economic base:**
- Granite & stone products (legacy industry)
- Manufacturing & light industrial
- Service & information technology businesses
- Diverse business development ecosystem

**Town initiatives:**
- Tax exemption programs for new industrial construction (adopted 2018)
- Economic Revitalization Zones with state tax credits
- Active Community & Economic Development office
- Business-friendly zoning and development approvals

**Lifestyle anchors:**
- **Ogie Brewing Co.** — locally sourced, elevated pub food, craft beer
- **Milford Recreation Department** — extensive programming, parks, community events
- **Route 101 corridor** — convenient commute to I-93 (Nashua, Boston)
