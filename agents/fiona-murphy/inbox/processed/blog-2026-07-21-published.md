## Post Content (450+ words)

The real estate market isn't one size fits all. In Hillsborough County, 2026 has created a fascinating split: some towns are burning hot, while others offer unexpected opportunity.

Here's what the data tells us.

### The Overall Picture
Hillsborough County is up 11 percent in active listings compared to last year. That's the highest June total since 2021. At the same time, homes are still selling in just 23 days on average, half the national pace. Prices are holding steady with a median of 548,453 dollars and 62 percent of homes selling above asking.

But zoom in, and the story gets much more interesting.

### The Hot Tier: Below $500K (Nashua, Manchester, Hooksett)
This is where competition is fiercest. Demand still vastly outpaces supply. If you're a seller with a home under 500K dollars, you likely have multiple offers. If you're a buyer in this tier, you need to be prepared, move fast, and know your numbers.

Nashua leads the county with consistent volume and strong buyer demand. These homes are priced for accessibility, and they're priced for speed.

### The Balanced Tier: $500K–$900K (Amherst, Hollis, Bedford, Bow)
This is where the sweet spot lives. Inventory growth has given buyers breathing room for the first time in years. You're not in a buyer's market, but you have leverage. Negotiation is back on the table.

Amherst sits in this sweet spot with excellent schools and suburban appeal. Hollis offers character and space. Buyers here can actually explore their options without panic.

### The Prestige Tier: $800K+ (Mont Vernon, select Hollis addresses)
Mont Vernon has become the crown jewel of the Southern NH market. The median listing price here is 799K dollars, nearly 250K dollars above the county median. Why? Schools are top 5 percent in the state, town character is exceptional, and there's proximity to everything: Boston, lakes, hiking, and the new Daland Memorial Library opening in 2026.

For relocating professionals from Boston or the tech corridor, Mont Vernon is the aspirational anchor.

### Why Speed Still Matters
At 23 days to sold, Southern NH homes move faster than almost anywhere in New England. This speed signal means several things:

First, your home has to be priced right from day one. There's no room for wishful thinking.

Second, buyer preparation wins. The fastest sales go to the most ready buyers: those with financing in place, inspection timelines planned, and expectations clear.

Third, your market position matters. In the sub-500K tier, you're competing with 15 other listings. In the 500K to 900K tier, you're competing with five. In the prestige tier, you're in a different league entirely.

### What to Do
If you're selling: Know your tier. Price it right. Get it market-ready fast.

If you're buying: Identify your tier. Understand the competition level. Get prepared before you search.

Location matters. Timing matters. But strategy matters most.

The Hillsborough County heat map tells the story. Are you reading it correctly?
