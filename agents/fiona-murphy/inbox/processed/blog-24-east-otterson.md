- 4 two-bedroom units (1,530 sq ft)
- 1 studio unit (590 sq ft)
- 30 enclosed parking spaces on ground level
- Full architectural/engineering plans and Phase 21E environmental clearance convey with the sale

This is a shovel-ready development parcel — a rare find in downtown Nashua.

### Location Highlights
- Downtown Nashua — walkability score ~95 (Walker's Paradise)
- 2 blocks from Southern New Hampshire Medical Center (188-bed hospital, 500+ staff)
- Adjacent to Mine Falls Park (325 acres — trails, fishing, kayaking, soccer)
- Walking distance to Elm Street restaurants, breweries, Nash Casino, Nashua Center for the Arts
- Historic Millyard district (on National Register of Historic Places)
- Route 3 access within 5 minutes
- Public water, sewer, cable all at the street

### Schools (Nashua SD)

- Strong public school system citywide

### Why This Matters for the NH Market
- Aligns with Nashua's housing goals under the *Imagine Nashua* Master Plan
- NH has a significant housing supply shortage — urban infill like this is exactly what's needed
- D3MU zoning is rare and valuable — very few lots of this size remain in downtown Nashua
- Healthcare worker housing demand is strong with SNHMC 2 blocks away
