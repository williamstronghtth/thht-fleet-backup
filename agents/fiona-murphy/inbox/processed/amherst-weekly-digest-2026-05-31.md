# Amherst, NH Weekly News Digest
**Week of May 31 – June 6, 2026**

---

## 🏠 Real Estate Market Snapshot

**Market Conditions (May 2026)**
- **Median Home Price:** $739K (as of May 2026)
- **Average Home Value:** $594,844 (+5.7% YoY)
- **Average Price Last Month:** $660K (+39.8% YoY)
- **Price per Sq. Ft.:** $353 (+13.3% YoY)
- **Market Competitiveness Score:** 77/100 (Very Competitive)
- **Days on Market (Broader NH):** 44 days (up from 32 in 2024 — more time for buyer decisions)
- **Homes Selling Above List:** 35.6% still command premiums
- **Sale-to-List Ratio:** 99.1% (continues to favor sellers)

**Key Insight for Sellers:** While the NH market is softening slightly compared to 2023-24 peaks, Amherst remains competitive with strong pricing power. The increase in days on market (44 vs. 32) gives sellers more breathing room for strategic positioning but also suggests the buyer pool is being more selective. The 99.1% sale-to-list ratio is still favorable.

## 📰 Town News & Announcements

### Current Government Initiatives
- **2026 Full Statistical Revaluation:** The Town of Amherst has contracted with Vision Government Solutions, Inc. to conduct a comprehensive community revaluation — all homeowners should expect valuation notices in the coming months.
- **Recreation Department Update:** The Rec Department took over management of the beloved Amherst Tree Lighting, now under community stewardship.
- **Transfer Station Policy:** The town implemented a cashless-only policy at the Transfer Station, streamlining operations.
