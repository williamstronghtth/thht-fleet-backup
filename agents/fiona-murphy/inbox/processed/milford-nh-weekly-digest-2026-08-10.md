## 1. Market Snapshot

**Milford, NH (town-level):**
- **Median sale price:** ~$500K–$510K range (Redfin ~$502K late spring, up ~9.8% YoY; July median list price near $589K signals sellers testing the top).
- **Days on market:** ~34 days — moving, but slightly slower than county pace.
- **Competition:** ~2 offers/home; well-priced move-in-ready listings still draw multiple bids.
- **Price range on market:** ~$249K to $1.3M — genuine entry-level to luxury spread.

**Hillsborough County backdrop (July 2026):**
- Median ~**$548,392** | **1.71 months supply** (seller's market under 6mo) | **24-day DOM** county-wide.
- **Pending sales down ~1%** — first negative reading, an early cooling signal.
- **30-yr mortgage ~6.69–6.76%**, **5th straight weekly uptick**.

**The read:** Milford remains a seller's market, but momentum is shifting. Tight supply supports prices; rising rates + first pending-sales dip mean pricing discipline matters more than three months ago. Overshooting sellers (that ~$589K list vs ~$502K sold gap) risk sitting past the 34-day mark.
