- **Median listing price:** $739K (May 2026)
- **Average home value:** $594,844 (up 5.7% YoY)
- **Days on market:** 32 days
- **Market competition score:** 77/100 (very competitive)

### Market Context
Amherst reflects broader NH trends: the state's median single-family home price reached $530K in March (+1% YoY), showing stabilization after recent volatility. The Amherst market remains seller-favorable with strong pricing power, though inventory is gradually improving compared to 2024-2025.

**Prospecting angle:** Homes selling in ~1 month suggest motivated buyers in the current rate environment. New construction and move-up opportunities remain strong for premium inventory.
