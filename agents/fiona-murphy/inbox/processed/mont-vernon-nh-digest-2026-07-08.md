## 2. Town Character: What Makes Mont Vernon Distinctive in July

Mont Vernon is at its best in summer. The conservation lands are fully accessible — Purgatory Falls and the Wah Lum Loop reward visitors with waterfall views and old-growth forest within minutes of residential neighborhoods. The town moves slowly in July; there are no festivals competing for parking, no tourist traffic, no crowds. That's the point.

**The defining facts for any buyer conversation:**

- Population ~2,800, density ~145 people per square mile — genuinely rural
- No stoplights, no chain retail, no downtown commercial district (by design)
- Mont Vernon Village School: #11 of 224 NH elementary schools, 85% math proficiency vs. 42% statewide, 12:1 student-teacher ratio — the single most important driver of the price premium
- Daland Memorial Library opened February 2026 after a 40-year community effort; ~500 opening-day visitors (1 in 5 residents) — the clearest signal of how invested this community is in itself
- 20 minutes to Manchester, 30 to Nashua, ~70–90 minutes to Boston; no state income tax
- School district note: K–6 at Mont Vernon Village School; grades 7–12 at Milford Middle and Milford High (SAU 39)

**The July milestone:** The America 250 community celebration happened July 4, 2026 — Chris's fourth day as a Mont Vernon resident. The town honored its 46 Revolutionary War veterans. Chris was there as a neighbor, not as a visitor. That is a story.

## 3. Content Hooks for Social / Blog

### Hook 1: "One Week In — What It's Actually Like to Live in Mont Vernon"
Chris has been a Mont Vernon resident for one week. This is the moment to capture raw, first-person impressions before they become routine.
- What did he notice that he didn't expect? How do neighbors greet you? What did the Fourth look like from the inside?
- **Format:** Instagram Reel or YouTube short (2–3 min), Chris to camera, casual and honest
- **CTA:** "I moved here so I could tell you what it's actually like. DM me your questions."
- **Why now:** The "just moved in" window closes fast. Authenticity is highest in week one.

### Hook 2: "The Town That Honored 46 Revolutionary War Heroes on the 250th"
The America 250 celebration in Mont Vernon was hyperlocal history — a small town with a disproportionate Revolutionary War legacy marking the national anniversary.
- The hook: most towns do fireworks. Mont Vernon does history. That tells you something about the residents.
- **Format:** Short blog post + Instagram carousel (town images, historical context, community gathering feel)
- **CTA:** "This is the kind of place where history isn't just a museum. It's the neighborhood."
- **Tie-in:** Links naturally to the "why we moved here" narrative.

### Hook 3: "The School Stat No One Talks About Out Loud"
Most buyers have heard Mont Vernon has a good school. Most don't know that 85% of students are proficient in math versus 42% statewide — a gap that doubles the NH average in a public school.
- Lead with the number, unpack what it means (community investment, small class sizes, 12:1 ratio), land on the value implication.
- **Format:** Instagram stat card (the number big, context underneath) + blog post for SEO
- **SEO target:** "Mont Vernon NH elementary school" / "best schools southern NH"
- **CTA:** "Here's why families pay a premium to be in this zip code."

### Hook 4: "Southern NH Summer — What $650K Buys in a Town With No Stoplights"
A listing-style walkthrough of what $600K–$700K buys in Mont Vernon right now: acreage, newer construction, privacy, access to trails — compared to what that same budget gets in Nashua or even Amherst.
- This is the "context carousel" that performs well as a pinned post.
- **Format:** Instagram carousel (5–6 slides: price → lot size → school → trails → commute → bottom line)
- **CTA:** "Tell me your budget and I'll show you what it buys in Mont Vernon."
- **Note:** Acknowledge when using estimated figures; encourage buyers to request a real comp pull.

### Hook 5: "Why a Boston-Area Family Saves $15,000+ a Year by Moving Here"
NH has no state income tax and no sales tax. A household earning $180K moving from Massachusetts saves meaningfully every year — enough to materially affect what mortgage they can carry.
- Mont Vernon is a Boston-corridor town by commute (70–90 min) with southern NH pricing and NH tax advantages.
- **Format:** LinkedIn post (targets professionals) + email newsletter insert
- **CTA:** "Want to run the actual math on your move? Let's talk."
