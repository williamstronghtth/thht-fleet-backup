## The Concept

Short-form series: Chris shows 3-4 visual clues from a specific Souhegan Valley town — no labels, no voiceover — and asks: *"Which town is this? Drop your guess below."*

Next video (3 days later): reveal + 60-second story on the one detail that makes that town unmistakably itself.

## Why It Works

- Guess formats generate 3-5x more comments than standard videos
- Chris plays KoalaKlub GeoSports — this is literally his hobby translated to content
- Proves local knowledge through specificity, not claims ("I've been here 20 years")
- Series writes itself for a year: one town per week
