## Market Snapshot — Southern NH / Hillsborough County

**Statewide (NH):**
- Median sale price: ~$500,200 (March 2026), up 0.2% YoY — slowest appreciation since May 2023
- Average days on market: 29–31 days
- Months of supply: ~1.5 (well below the 6-month balanced-market threshold)
- Sellers getting ~100% of list price — still a seller's market
- NH ranked #8 Hottest Real Estate Market in the U.S. for 2026

**Hillsborough County specifically:**
- Median home price: ~$500K–$540K (Q1 2026)
- Inventory: 1.4 months supply — extreme seller's market
- Avg days on market: 36 days, ~2 offers per home
- Price forecast: modest 2–4% appreciation through 2026

**Local towns (core farm area):**
- Mont Vernon: Median listing ~$749K — small-town charm, highly desirable
- Amherst: Avg ~$594K — family-friendly, strong schools
- Milford: Avg ~$443K — more accessible entry-point, growing demand
- Nashua & Salem: Active demand, cross-border MA commuters a key buyer segment
