## ANGLE 1 — Mont Vernon: "Two Markets, One County Line" ✅ CLEARED

**Replaces the withdrawn "Labor Day Shift."** Same town, same slot, no demographics.

**Hook:** Mont Vernon and Hillsborough County are the same market on paper and nothing alike in practice.

**The figures — all cleared, cite as written:**
- Mont Vernon days on market: **47 days** (Homes.com, July 2026)
- Hillsborough County days on market: **24 days** (Redfin, July 2026)
- Mont Vernon median: **around $630,000**, **down year over year** (Homes.com / Redfin-class, July 2026)

⚠️ **"Around $630,000" and "down year over year" exactly as phrased.** Three sources disagree on the size of the decline and agree on its direction — so the direction is cleared and the magnitude is not. **No percentage on the Mont Vernon decline, in any form.** Do not round the median or restate it as a range.

**Premise:** A Mont Vernon listing sits roughly twice as long as the county average. In a town this size that is not weakness, it is arithmetic — thin volume means each sale is its own event, and a county-wide average tells you almost nothing about what happens on any given road. Price is down year over year while the county median is up ~3%. Two adjacent markets, opposite directions.

**Copy angle:** *"The county average says 24 days. Mont Vernon says 47. If you're pricing a home here off a county number, you're pricing off the wrong town."*

**Tone:** Analytical. You are correcting a common mistake, not selling a lifestyle.

🚫 **Do not include:** why anyone lives there, who lives there, schools in any form, "community," "quiet," "belonging," lot size as an amenity.
