## The Viral Outlier Principle

The Villages video (490K views in Year 1) is an outlier — it's a **destination piece** about a famous Florida phenomenon that draws national viewers, not just local ones.

**For Chris's NH equivalent — ideas:**
- "What it's like to live through a NH winter as a Floridian" (his literal story)
- Deep-dive on the AMC huts system
- Old Man of the Mountain / White Mountains history
- "Why everyone in New England is obsessed with foliage" (explainer for outsiders)
- Mont Vernon's own quirks (the school that drew him there, etc.)
- Peterborough — birthplace of "Our Town"
- The NH Advantage (no income/sales tax — every out-of-stater asks about this)

One unexpected viral piece can shortcut audience-building dramatically. **Recommend: plan 1-2 destination/outlier pieces in the first 12-month calendar.**
