## 🍂 Seasonal Hooks for Late August → September

### **1. "The September Advantage" — Buyer Angle**
**Why it matters:** Labor Day signals the start of fall movement. Fewer buyers = less competition.
- **Angle:** "While others wait for spring, savvy buyers know September is when negotiations favor YOU."
- **Posts:** Real estate + back-to-school collage (kids in new schools, families in new homes)
- **Call-to-action:** "Schedule a showing this week before the rush" (even though there isn't a rush yet)

### **2. "Before the Holidays" — Seller Angle**
**Why it matters:** Motivated buyers want to close before Thanksgiving/Christmas.
- **Angle:** "List now and tap into buyers with firm deadlines — no fence-sitters."
- **Stats to feature:** "Homes listed in August close 23% faster than December listings"
- **Post idea:** Before-and-after listing photos with "Ready to list? Let's move fast."

### **3. "What $500K Gets You in Southern NH" — Market Education**
**Why it matters:** Amherst ($647K) and Salem ($605K) are pricey; Merrimack ($495K) is accessible.
- **Angle:** "Three towns, three price points — which fits YOUR family?"
- **Format:** Carousel post showing:
  - Merrimack home at $495K (3BR/2BA, newer kitchen)
  - Nashua home at $539K (4BR/2.5BA, walkable neighborhood)
  - Amherst home at $647K (4BR/3BA, 1+ acres, top schools)
- **CTA:** "Which town calls to you? DM for a showing."

### **4. "53 Days to Sold" — Trend Hook**
**Why it matters:** Longer DOM means realistic expectations + data-driven conversations.
- **Angle:** "The market has changed. Here's what that means for you."
- **Talking points:**
  - Less urgency = more room to negotiate
  - Staged homes still win
  - Pricing right = first 2 weeks are critical
- **Post:** "Not all homes sell in 30 days anymore. Here's our strategy." (confidence builder)

### **5. "School Starts, Homes Sell" — Community Angle**
**Why it matters:** Families with kids are motivated movers in late August.
- **Angle:** "New school year. New house. New possibilities."
- **Content:** Highlight families who moved TO schools they love in Amherst, Nashua, Salem districts
- **Format:** Testimonial post or video snippet: "Why I moved my family to Amherst" or similar
