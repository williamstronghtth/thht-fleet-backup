## Critical Insight #1: Luxury Numbers Are Skewed

Luxury Home Tour shows 116K avg views — but that's heavily skewed by:
- **Shaq's Mansion Tour: 2,000,000 views** (his #1 video by a mile)

Without these celebrity/destination outliers, luxury home tours average much lower. **Don't chase luxury content early** — you need the access first, and the access comes after the audience.

## The Viral Outlier Principle

Pozek's #1 video (Shaq, 2M views) came from **unique access** — a celebrity connection that no other real estate agent had.

His #2 video (The Villages, 490K) came from **destination curiosity** — people wondering what a famous community actually looks like.

Every channel has a potential viral outlier. For the NH channel, candidates:
1. **"I Moved My Family from Florida to NH — Here's What Nobody Tells You"** (Chris's literal life story, July 2026)
2. **A famous NH property tour** — Lake Winnipesaukee lakefront, a historic New England estate
3. **"The Truth About NH Winters (from a Floridian)"** — destination curiosity + relocation search traffic

The outlier doesn't need to be planned. It needs to be available when the moment comes.
