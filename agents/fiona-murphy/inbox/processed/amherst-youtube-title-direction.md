## Core Facts to Cover in the Script

Here's what I'd anchor the script around — this should give you what you need for SEO alignment and the full packaging package:

1. **School system** — Top-ranked in NH; families relocating from MA often cite this as #1 reason. Be specific: Souhegan High School, Wilkins Elementary, etc.
2. **Price point** — ~$595K average, up 5.7% YoY. Not the cheapest entry but a clear step up in character and land from Nashua/Milford.
3. **Lot sizes and housing stock** — Bigger lots (1–5 acres common), Colonials, New Englands, some newer construction. Not condo-heavy.
4. **Boston commute reality** — ~45 min to Mass border via Rt 101 W. No NH income tax. Strong selling point for MA relocators.
5. **32-day DOM** — Slower than Nashua (12 days), which is a buyer advantage worth calling out directly.
6. **Town center / character** — Historic town center, village feel, not a suburb strip-mall vibe.
7. **The "misunderstood" angle** — Amherst doesn't market itself well to out-of-staters. Most people find it only after ruling out Nashua or Bedford. Position Chris as the guide who reveals it.
