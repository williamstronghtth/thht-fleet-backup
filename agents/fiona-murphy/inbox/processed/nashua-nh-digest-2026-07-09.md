## 1. Market Snapshot

### Prices
- **Nashua median sale price:** ~$490,000–$514,000 (sources vary; use ~$500K as the clean round figure)
- **Year-over-year appreciation:** +5.6% to +10.6% depending on source and timeframe — call it "5–10% YoY" in copy
- **Price per sq ft:** ~$292 (flat YoY, which is a healthy stabilization story, not a decline)
- **Condo/townhouse median:** ~$400,000 (April 2026) — meaningfully more accessible entry point
- **Sale-to-list ratio:** 100.4–100.7% — sellers are getting asking price or better

### Days on Market
- **Nashua DOM:** ~18 days (internal THHT data; consistent with Q1/Q2 2026 market behavior showing 11–18 day medians)
- **Hillsborough County DOM:** ~44 days (up from 30–32 days in spring 2024)
- **The gap:** Nashua sells in less than half the county average time — a 59% faster pace

### Inventory
- **Supply:** Extremely constrained. Nashua has operated near 0.29 months of supply in recent readings — vs. 6 months for a balanced market
- **New construction pipeline:** ~1,918 new housing units approved or under construction in Nashua; 359 completed since mid-2024
- **Notable incoming supply:** Doucet Landing (South Nashua) — 83 new condos, expected completion end of 2026
- **Net effect:** New supply is arriving but not fast enough to meaningfully relieve buyer pressure at the sub-$500K level

### Market Direction
- **Verdict: Strong seller's market with selective buyer opportunities**
- Under $500K: Multiple offers, waived contingencies still happening (though less than 2025)
- Over $500K: More negotiating room; buyers have options
- Prices are stabilizing (not crashing, not spiking) — the "soft landing" story is real
- Mortgage rates: 6.664% as of July 6, 2026. Hawkish Fed; rates likely 6.45–6.65% through rest of 2026

Use these to differentiate Nashua in any context — listing presentations, buyer consultations, social DMs, blog intros.

| Metric | Nashua | Hillsborough County Avg |
|---|---|---|
| Median Sale Price | ~$500K | ~$540K (Q1 2026) |
| Days on Market | 18 days | 44 days |
| Sale-to-List Ratio | 100.4–100.7% | Below asking in slower towns |
| YoY Appreciation | +5.6–10.6% | Mixed; county down 1.7% Q1 |
| New Construction | 1,918 units in pipeline | Minimal comparable activity |
| Income Tax | $0 (NH) | $0 (NH) — but worth stating vs. MA |

**The narrative in one sentence:** Nashua is the market in Hillsborough County where homes move fastest, appreciation is strongest, and new supply is actually arriving — making it the most dynamic sub-market for both buyers and sellers right now.

**What the county average obscures:** When people see "44 days on market" for Hillsborough County and feel cautious, they're averaging slow-moving rural towns and luxury-tier properties with Nashua's fast-turn inventory. Nashua's 18-day DOM is not a county story. It's a city story. That's the correction to make every time.
