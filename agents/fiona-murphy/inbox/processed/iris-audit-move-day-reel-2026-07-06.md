## The specific concept

**Format:** Talking-head Reel, 45–60 seconds. Shot on iPhone, front porch or backyard. No production. The rough edges are the point.

**Hook (on-screen text + spoken):** *"I moved to New Hampshire one week ago. Here's what nobody told me about Amherst."*

**Body:** 3 things — one funny, one warm, one real. NOT market data. Examples:
- "I thought I knew New Hampshire. I've driven every road in this valley three times. I was still wrong about [something specific]."
- "My neighbor three houses down had already knocked on our door by Tuesday. That does not happen in Florida."
- "There is no traffic light in this town. I've checked. Zero."

**Close (spoken, directly to camera):** *"This is the town I'm selling homes in. It's also where I'm going to sleep tonight. Those two things aren't usually the same thing. In the Souhegan Valley, they are for me now."*

**Caption:**
> Week 1 in NH. I got here last Tuesday. By Friday I knew every coffee shop name and half the neighbors on our street. That's Amherst. If you've ever thought about making a move like this — to a town where people actually know each other — let's talk. Link in bio. 🏡
> #NewHampshire #SouheganValley #AmherstNH #MovedToNH #MoveToNH

---

## Why this works

- **No footage required.** Chris walks outside with his phone. This can happen today or tomorrow.
- **Kills the generic.** Nobody else in NH real estate has this story. "I'm a Florida agent who moved to the market I now serve" is a differentiator that no amount of data can replicate.
- **Algorithm-friendly.** Talking heads with specific detail ("zero traffic lights") outperform B-roll montages for comments and shares. People tag friends and say *"this is so Amherst."*
- **Cross-feed with Guess the Town.** If yesterday's Spark lands, Episode 1 is Amherst. This Reel feeds right into that series — it's the "why I know this place" backstory.
