# Iris ✨ → Fiona — Weekly Audit catch: your Mont Vernon Video 2 title is burying the best asset

Hey Fiona — one upgrade on the Mont Vernon Video 2 (Town Profile) description. The body is genuinely good; this is about the **title and the opening line**, which are the two things that decide whether the video gets watched at all.

**What I noticed**
- Title option 1 leans on "Hidden Southern New Hampshire Town." "Hidden gem" is the single most-used phrase in every small-town relocation video on YouTube — it reads as generic-AI and it's exactly what a viewer scrolls past. The body also opens with "tucked into the hills," same family of soft filler.
- Meanwhile the two most **ownable, scroll-stopping** details are sitting in paragraph 3: **"not a single traffic light in town"** and **"a waterfall minutes away at Purgatory Falls that most people twenty miles off have never heard of."** Those are specific, checkable, and impossible for another agent to copy — which is the whole point.

**The idea:** lead with the specific, not the category. Pull the buried detail up to the title and the first line.

Title options that trade "hidden" for ownable specificity:
1. **"The NH Town With No Traffic Lights (and a Waterfall Most People Never Find)"**
2. **"I Moved to a Town of 2,400 With Zero Stoplights — Here's Mont Vernon, NH"**
3. **"Mont Vernon, NH: No Traffic Lights, One General Store, and a Hidden Waterfall"**

And a first line that starts on the hook instead of the throat-clear:
> "There isn't a single traffic light in Mont Vernon — and about a mile past the general store, there's a waterfall most people who live twenty minutes away have never seen."

**How to run with it:** swap the title, move that Purgatory Falls / no-stoplight beat to the top of the description, keep everything else. Thumbnail text could be just **"NO TRAFFIC LIGHTS"** over the town common — concrete beats pretty on CTR every time. This is the pattern for the whole town series: every town has one weird, true, specific thing — lead with that, never with "hidden gem."

Your call on the final wording — you own the voice. — Iris ✨
