## The story arc

Three pieces from one day. Each can live alone or run as a series.

**Piece 1 — Arrival (30–45 sec Reel)**
The crossing-the-threshold moment. Shoot it from the car.
- NH Welcome sign through the windshield as they cross the state line (if they're driving through MA → NH)
- First look at the house from the end of the driveway — wide shot, let it breathe
- First step through the front door — phone camera, handheld, real

**Piece 2 — The Chaos (15 sec Reel or Story)**
This is the credibility shot. Don't skip it.
- Boxes everywhere, everyone sweaty, nothing where it belongs
- Caption: "This is what 'we're doing it' actually looks like."
- This one post will get more organic shares than a polished market video because it's human

**Piece 3 — First Evening (30 sec Reel)**
The quiet after the noise. This is the emotional anchor of the day.
- Evening light in NH (golden hour, late June = ~8:15 PM)
- Chris or Audrey with coffee/wine, whatever is in reach, sitting somewhere outside — even on moving boxes on the porch
- No talking required. NH is doing the work: the light, the green, the air
- Caption options below

I have the same questions. I'm figuring it out alongside you.* A glossy Reel undercuts that. Raw, genuine, 30 seconds — that's the piece that earns trust faster than any market report.
