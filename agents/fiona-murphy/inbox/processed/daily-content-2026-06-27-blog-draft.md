## Blog Post Content

The Southern New Hampshire real estate market is at an inflection point. After years of intense demand and limited inventory, the market is rebalancing, creating distinct opportunities for both buyers and sellers.

Here's what the data shows and what it means for you.

### The Market Snapshot

Hillsborough County Q1 2026 median: $540,917, down 1.7% year over year. May listing price: $585,000. At first glance, these numbers might sound like a slowdown. They're actually a reset. A necessary breath after years of frenzy. The real story lies deeper.

### The Million-Dollar Boom

Here's the number that matters most: 373% increase in million-dollar sales over the last five years in Hillsborough County. That's exponential growth, not decline. Boston relocators are moving to Nashua, Amherst, Mont Vernon, and beyond, seeking space, schools, and lifestyle. They're bringing capital. They're driving competition at the high end.

Your home's equity is real. And it's growing.

### The Buyer's Moment

For the first time in years, buyers have leverage. Inspection waivers are reversing. Negotiation room is returning. Smart buyers who move now, with the right strategy, will win.

The shift is real. Homes that would have sold in multiple offers six months ago are sitting on the market for a few extra days. That's your window. Rates are stabilizing around 6.2 to 6.4 percent, meaning your purchasing power is predictable. Inventory is growing, giving you actual choices instead of panic buying.

### The Seller's Playbook

Pandemic-era tactics are dead. Generic pricing, minimal marketing, and listing blindfolded to competition no longer work.

What does? Honest comp-driven pricing on day one. Professional photography. A deliberate 10-day marketing push.

Underpricing to move fast beats holding out for a higher number that never comes. Yes, Hillsborough County has cooled, but that doesn't mean your home isn't worth what you think. It means smart pricing moves faster. Transparent, data-driven pricing wins in this market every single time.

### What This Means for You

If you're buying: Prepare, but don't panic. Rates are holding around 6.2 to 6.4 percent. Inventory is growing. You have time and options.

If you're selling: Act. The market rewards action. List it right, price it right, and move it fast. Hesitation costs money.
