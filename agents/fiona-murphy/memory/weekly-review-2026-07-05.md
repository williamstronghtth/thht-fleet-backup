## Weekly Narrative Theme

This week's content was anchored to a single, consistent market story: **the Southern NH buyer's market is back.** After three years of seller dominance, data now supports a genuine shift.

**Key data points repeated across posts:**
- Days on market: 44 (up from 32 in spring)
- Active inventory: +13.3% YoY (6,007 homes)
- Hillsborough County median price: $540,917 (down 1.7% YoY)
- Amherst median: down 35% — clear opportunity zone

This week's framing was careful: "buyer's market" was positioned as breathing room and opportunity, not alarm or desperation. The tone stayed polished, confident, and market-educated throughout.
