## Session — 14:14 ET (Mont Vernon Research)
- **What was done:** Researched Mont Vernon history for Chris: founding story, why no U in the name, population (2,715), median home value ($669K), school ratings, and the new Daland Memorial Library.
- **Key facts captured:**
  - Name: French "mont" (not English "mount") — used from day one in 1803 charter, intentional per Historical Society
  - Hotel owner George Kittredge added the U via Post Office in late 1800s; reverted after his death
  - Incorporated 1803 after splitting from Amherst; early settlers arrived ~1740
  - Population: 2,715 (2026 projection)
  - Median home value: $669,000
